## Chapter 1320-08-19 Trip Permits New
# Chapter 1320-08-19 Trip Permits New

Rule 1320-8-19-.01 Applicable Fee is a new rule. All subsequent rules, and references thereto, are renumbered accordingly.

## 1320-8-19-.01 APPLICABLE FEE.

The fee for trip permits issued under T.C.A. § 55-4-115(b)(1) shall be $30.00, as such amount is comparable to the fee imposed for like permits by other jurisdictions.

Authority: T.C.A. §§ 55-2-103, 55-4-101, 55-4-115, 4-3-1903, 67-1-102, and Executive Order 36 (2006).
