# MA: permission request

**To:** Secretary of the Commonwealth, Regulations Division (State Publications and Regulations).
Find the address on the Division's contact page; none was confirmed during the rights check.
**Subject:** Permission request: automated reading of public hearing notices for a civic deadline service

Hello,

I'm building DocketClock, a service that helps members of the public find state and federal public
comment periods and hearings and see their deadlines, with a link back to the official source.

Your website's terms prohibit scraping and crawling, so I'm writing to ask permission before doing
anything automated. Specifically, I'd like to:

- read the public hearings and notices pages and their attached notices, at most one request every
  5 seconds, with a user agent that identifies the service and a contact address;
- keep a private copy of each notice so our dates can be checked against the original;
- show the public only the hearing or deadline details, a short quoted sentence that sets them, and
  a link to your page. We would not republish the notices.

If automated access isn't acceptable, is there another route you'd prefer, such as a feed, an
e-mail list or a bulk delivery? We'd also like to know whether the Massachusetts Register
subscription terms allow this use.
