Purpose of the Proposal and Its Anticipated Effects, Including Any Changes in Existing Rules: The purpose of this rule making is to comply with RCW 66.24.240(7) and 66.24.244(8), which require the state board of health (board) to adopt rules to allow dogs to be present in domestic breweries and microbreweries that are not subject to food service permits. This proposal will require a new subsection to be added to chapter 246-203 WAC, General sanitation.
Reasons Supporting Proposal: RCW 66.24.240(7) and 66.24.244(8) require the board to "adopt rules to allow dogs on the premises of licensed domestic breweries that do not provide food service subject to a food service permit requirement." The 2025 legislature passed EHB 1602 (chapter 141, Laws of 2025) that amended multiple statutes to allow domestic breweries and microbreweries to provide food service via contract kitchens. A contract kitchen may take the form of a mobile food service, such as a food truck, or other independently operated food service provider. The bill was codified as RCW 66.24.240, 66.24.244, 66.24.320, 66.24.410, 66.04.010, and 66.24.400, and became effective July 27, 2025.
These amendments require changes to board rules allowing a domestic brewery or microbrewery that doesn't provide food service themselves or via a contract kitchen to allow pet dogs to be present on site.
Statutory Authority for Adoption: RCW 66.24.240 and 66.24.244 require the board to adopt rules regarding the presence of dogs in breweries and microbreweries that do not provide or contract for food service.    
Statute Being Implemented: The authorizing statutes and the statutes to be implemented are the same.

RDS-7373.1
NEW SECTION
WAC 246-203-015Dogs in breweries and micro breweries that are not licensed food service establishments.
(1) This section applies to domestic breweries licensed under RCW 66.24.240 and microbreweries licensed under RCW 66.24.244 that do not provide or subcontract for food service subject to a food service permit under chapter 246-215 WAC.
(2) A domestic brewery or microbrewery that does not provide or subcontract for food service subject to a food service permit under chapter 246-215 WAC may choose to allow pet dogs to be present on the premises.
(3) A domestic brewery or microbrewery may not prohibit a patron from bringing a service animal as defined by RCW 49.60.040 on the premises.
