Purpose of the Proposal and Its Anticipated Effects, Including Any Changes in Existing Rules: The purpose is to give the college discretion on designating an administrator to serve as the public records officer and updating the contact information for the public records officer.

Reasons Supporting Proposal: The college has discretion on designating an administrator as the public records officer rather than identifying a specific administrator as the public records officer.

Statutory Authority for Adoption: RCW  28B.50.140,  42.56.040, and  42.56.580.
