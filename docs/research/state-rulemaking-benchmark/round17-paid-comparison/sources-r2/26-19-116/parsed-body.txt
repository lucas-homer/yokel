Purpose of the Proposal and Its Anticipated Effects, Including Any Changes in Existing Rules: This rule making will repeal WAC 365-212-010 through 365-212-090.

Reasons Supporting Proposal: These rules are obsolete and inaccurate due to statute and programmatic changes since they were established in 2003. Incorrect information about the program and its operations can lead to confusion and potential legal risks since they do not reflect the current statutes.
