Purpose of the Proposal and Its Anticipated Effects, Including Any Changes in Existing Rules: The proposed new WAC 246-320-148: 

(1) Establishes an annual deadline of July 1st for hospitals to submit their price transparency data to the department in subsection; 

(2) Clarifies which data requirements may be submitted; and 

(3) Ties this reporting requirement to existing enforcement authority for hospitals licensed under chapter  70.41 RCW and chapter 246-320 WAC in subsection for clarity.

Subsection (2) of the proposed rule addresses confusion expressed by hospitals regarding state requirements under RCW  70.41.560 and federal price transparency requirements under 42 C.F.R. Part 180 by clarifying that the federal requirements, as they existed as of January 1, 2025, are the required minimum. However, any additional or different types of data elements beyond what was required as of January 1, 2025, by the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) may be included in the data submitted to the department. The intent is to clarify that data submitted to the department may meet the current or historical CMS requirements, as long as CMS has not removed reporting requirements. Changes made by CMS to 45 C.F.R. Part 180 since January 1, 2025, have only added data reporting elements. 

Reasons Supporting Proposal: The proposed new rule incorporates the requirements of RCW  70.41.560 while clarifying how the department will interpret if the hospitals have met them or not. Together, RCW  70.41.560 and the proposed new rule set a standard that will improve compliance, allow hospitals to report in a way that meets requirements of this state legislature as well as federal standards, and increase the amount of information available to the public regarding the costs for services and items. It also provides some flexibility to hospitals in an effort to reduce the burden of repetitive and slightly different reporting requirements. Rule making is necessary to provide enforceable minimum standards for the reporting of hospital price transparency and cost data while aligning with RCW  70.41.560.

RDS-7332.2

NEW SECTION

WAC 246-320-148Hospital price transparency reporting requirements.


(1) A hospital shall submit a machine-readable file containing the most recent list of all standard charges as described in RCW  70.41.560 to the department on an annual basis beginning July 1, 2027. All lists required under RCW  70.41.560 and this section must be submitted by July 1st of each year.

(2) To meet the requirements of subsection (1) of this section, a hospital may submit to the department a machine-readable file that meets the requirements for rules related to hospital price transparency pursuant to Title 45 C.F.R. Part 180, subparts A and B, if the information submitted is not less than what was required on January 1, 2025.

(3) The department may assess fines under WAC 246-320-013 against a hospital that fails to comply with this section.
