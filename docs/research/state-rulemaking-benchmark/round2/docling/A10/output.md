The Commission is also considering the Owner's request for a waiver of 16 NYCRR § 96.5(k)(3), which requires an energy audit for buildings where 20 percent or more of the residents receive income-based housing assistance.

The Owner states that, because the building is new construction subject to the current New York State Energy Conservation Construction Code, which provides strict energy conservation requirements for new and renovated buildings, including the design and construction of energy-efficient building envelopes, mechanical, lighting and power systems, an energy audit is not necessary.

## PROPOSED RULE MAKING NO HEARING(S) SCHEDULED

A Process for Offering Flexible Interconnection Service Options

I.D. No. PSC-39-26-00026-P

PURSUANT TO THE PROVISIONS OF THE State Administrative Procedure Act, NOTICE is hereby given of the following proposed rule:

Proposed Action: The Public Service Commission is considering the July 29, 2026, petition by New York State Electric &amp; Gas Corporation and Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation seeking approval to expand their flexible interconnection demonstration project.

Statutory authority: Public Service Law, sections 5, 65 and 66

Subject: A process for offering flexible interconnection service options.

Purpose: To provide rules that ensure safe and adequate service at just and reasonable rates.

Substance of proposed rule: The Public Service Commission (Commission) is considering the July 29, 2026, petition (Petition) by New York State Electric &amp; Gas Corporation and Rochester Gas and Electric Corporation (collectively, the Companies) seeking approval of their proposed tariff amendments to expand the Flexible Interconnection Capacity Solution (FICS) demonstration project. The Companies indicate that the proposed tariff language establishes the terms, conditions, and procedures for Flexible Interconnection applications for distributed energy resources (DERs) to interconnect at Company-identified locations. The Companies explain that the proposed expansion and tariff amendments are intended to enable increased utilization of existing distribution capacity by DERs while maintaining safe and reliable operation of the electric grid.

## PROPOSED RULE MAKING NO HEARING(S) SCHEDULED

Issuance of Securities and Other Forms of Indebtedness

I.D. No. PSC-39-26-00027-P

PURSUANT TO THE PROVISIONS OF THE State Administrative Procedure Act, NOTICE is hereby given of the following proposed rule:

Proposed Action: The Commission is considering a petition by National Fuel Gas Distribution Corporation for authority to issue long term debt in the aggregate principal amount not to exceed $500 million through December 31, 2029.

Statutory authority: Public Service Law, section 69

Subject: Issuance of securities and other forms of indebtedness.

Purpose: To provide funding for capital needs.

Substance of proposed rule: The Public Service Commission (Commission) is considering a petition of National Fuel Gas Distribution Corporation (NFG or the Company), filed on September 1, 2026, for authority to issue long-term securities for permitted purposes under Public Service Law Section 69.

NFG requests authority to issue up to $500 million of new longterm debt securities at any time through December 31, 2029, for purposes of (1) reimbursement of the Company's treasury for money expended for capital purposes, (2) payment for gross additions to utility plant used and useful in the public service, (3) repayment of shortterm debt obligations outstanding and long-term promissory notes issued by the Company that will mature in 2027 and 2028, and (4) other corporate purposes.
