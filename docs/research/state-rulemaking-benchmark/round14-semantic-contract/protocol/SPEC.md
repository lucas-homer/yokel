## Semantic obligations

Every deadline assertion explicitly preserves cutoff clock, timezone and receipt-versus-dispatch timeliness basis in c, including each unknown dimension. Date-only deadlines use nonzero q and specific r. Neither location nor endpoint supplies missing timezone, cutoff or receipt law. Preserve stated receipt or dispatch basis without fabricating the other dimensions. A source-established non-submission function can make receipt-versus-dispatch inapplicable; unknown function cannot silently imply inapplicability. This applies to deadline assertions, not every historical date. Independently meaningful date, clock and zone facts remain separate units while deadline qualifications stay attached to the date unit.

Inventory affected-rule identity separately from historical-filing identity. A transition explicitly targets the affected rule and links its identity, with historical filing separately linked as context; separate filing/effective dates preserve their respective anchors. d edges carry prerequisite/context links, not automatic normative force. The presence of a historical filing cannot substitute for the affected object.

Split independently lossy actor/object/phase, date/clock, city/province, rule/filing, endpoint component/role and qualification facts. Split at the level at which independently omitting or changing a fact loses source meaning; this is not punctuation splitting. Qualifications necessary to understand an assertion stay attached, even if separately inventoried. Multiple atomic units can share a broad exact evidence span. All independently stated background and unresolved functions survive; preserve transitions, proposed/deleted distinctions, exceptions and full source meaning.

Directive force follows source and context. A bare imperative or contact-by-date directive can be force-ambiguous; m5/q1/r preserves the actual action and uncertainty rather than forcing mandatory or advisory. Explicit modal wording is evidence within its scope, not a keyword oracle. Independently review uncertain legal/procedural force; an external reference disagreement alone cannot settle it. Retain all source-supported actor, purpose, applicability and conditions.

## Structural contract and host boundary

Envelope v,h,t,u,z and every required unit field i,k,s,m,c,d,b,e,q,r remain closed and required. Existing bounds remain: IDs1..160, fewer than160 units (160 stops for capacity review); strings s280/c180/r180; e1..6; d/b0..16. Roles k5 link first d to endpoint k4. Relationships k7 have at least two b members; other kinds have b=[]; d is acyclic, b is separate. Unknown modality requires nonzero q; q0 iff r empty. No coercion, default fill, clipping or repaired incomplete output.

Host splitlines(keepends=True) produces whole-line [ID,start,end,exact_text] rows over Unicode codepoints with exclusive ends. Full immutable source and complete span table are sent; exact reconstruction preserves Unicode, separators and bytes. schema.json binds the fictional source and table hashes. build(source) derives a new source-bound schema/table for another source without reading offline fixtures. Strict loading rejects duplicate/trailing/invalid JSON, nonfinite values and overflow; only finish_reason stop and closed complete END-delimited output validate. Source/table/version, field types/bounds, evidence IDs, link closure and graph rules are checked. expand resolves exact evidence and documented enums; contract verifies all restored fields and evidence against original source/table. Empty c reports no condition, not proven universal applicability.

Host checks do NOT decide prose entailment, atomicity, affected-object correctness, whether the deadline dimensions are expressed honestly, or directive force. Tests explicitly show semantically defective but structurally valid objects accepted by validate. No keyword heuristics claim to prove meaning. All expanded outputs retain completeness=unverified, production_eligible=false and action_ready=false. Exact evidence, all-spans coverage and terminal END do not prove completeness.
