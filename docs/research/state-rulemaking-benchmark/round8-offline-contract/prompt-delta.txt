
Timing relationship consistency: dependency_relation=none requires both anchor_event_ids and condition_requirement_ids to be empty. Relative or conditional timing must not use none. For before, after, upon, during, unless or other, supply at least one source-supported anchor event or condition requirement ID; IDs must reference inventory items. Use uncertain when the relationship cannot be established, keeping only supported IDs and expressing the unresolved relationship in uncertainties. Do not invent an anchor or remove supported bindings merely to satisfy validation. A non-calendar instruction such as "as soon as possible" retains its exact raw text and null value; use source-supported requirement-bound other or explicit uncertain as appropriate. Do not turn it into a calculated date, precise duration, cutoff, or asserted before-hearing relation. Keep "reasonable advance notice" separately supported without inventing how many days it means.
Coverage consistency: coverage.status is a structured source-review claim, not proof of completeness. Before choosing claimed_complete, check each participation function, qualification and source-supported detail against explicit events, timing, routes/components, requirements/disclosures, identities and uncertainties. Details present only inside evidence quotes do not count as structured assertions of those details. Preserve named accessibility aid formats and applicable recipient/contact associations in structured text with exact evidence. Use coverage.status=uncertain when representation is known to be incomplete or when you cannot establish whether all relevant facts, functions, qualifications and source uncertainties have been represented; name each coverage limitation in coverage.rationale with exact evidence and supported IDs in the uncertainties array. Faithfully and completely representing a genuine source ambiguity does not itself mean incomplete inventory coverage. Keep that ambiguity explicit in uncertainties and the applicable association/timing fields, with exact evidence, even if coverage is claimed_complete; its independent uncertainty review still applies. Never resolve an ambiguity or invent a binding merely to claim completeness. Do not claim completeness merely because the entire notice was read or many items were extracted. A completeness claim will still require independent verification; no asserted coverage status grants semantic acceptance.
Identity literal discipline: an email address is not evidence of a person's expanded name. Do not infer or expand a personal identity from an email local part. Preserve the exact printed name or endpoint and express any unestablished identity association as uncertainty; do not force a disputed or ambiguous association.
