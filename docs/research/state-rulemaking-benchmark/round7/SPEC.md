## Required behavior

### Dates

Extend the existing complete named-month grammar with an optional English weekday prefix (full or standard three-letter name, optional period/comma, whitespace), e.g. `Monday July 13, 2026`, `Fri., October 2, 2026`. Preserve exact `raw` and its resolved evidence. Normalize only the explicitly printed year/month/day to ISO date-only. Check any printed weekday against that complete Gregorian date; contradiction fails assembly with a distinct consistency error. Computing weekday consistency is validation of a printed date, not deriving a deadline. Existing ISO/month-first forms remain valid. Missing year, ambiguous numeric dates, invalid calendar dates, clock-qualified date strings and extra prose remain rejected as absolute-date literals; represent other timing kinds raw with null value. Do not infer year, timezone, relative deadline, precedence or date arithmetic.

### Atomic literals and presentation

Replace a route's single mandatory `literal` with required nonempty `components: [{id,kind,literal,evidence}]`; use bounded kinds `endpoint`, `name`, `address_part`, `meeting_id`, `passcode`, `activity`, `other`. Each component literal must occur contiguously in its own exact evidence. IDs are unique across the complete inventory, including components. Keep route-level evidence and event binding for the claimed relationship between components. This permits multiline addresses and credentials without claiming that a synthesized comma/semicolon string was printed. An oral route may use literal `orally` with a separately audited description. Identity literals remain exact and timing raw remains exact.

An optional nullable route `description` is a semantic claim with route evidence, audited for support/association; it is never an endpoint or substitute literal. Do not accept model-supplied arbitrary `display` text. If a display view is provided, generate it deterministically from ordered component literals using a fixed documented delimiter; retain component IDs and mark it derived presentation. Display contributes no new fact, evidence or source-match credit, and must be recomputable exactly (mutated display fails payload validation). Do not derive unprinted labels, address rearrangements or a submission endpoint from presentation. Verifiers receive all component evidence and relationship claims, with no duplicate raw inventory or optional display expansion in the common payload. Missing required or nonliteral components invalidate the whole assembly. Exact component resolution cannot algorithmically prove common route ownership: source-supported components attached to the wrong activity are semantic association failures, tested by independent authored mappings and verifier controls.

### Alternative groups and applicability

Replace route `alternative_group` strings with `alternative_group_id: string|null` and a required top-level `alternative_groups` array. Each group has globally unique `id`, `purpose`, `route_ids` (at least two distinct members), `event_ids`, `requirement_ids`, `association: source_resolved|uncertain`, and exact `evidence`. Purposes: `submission`, `hearing_registration`, `hearing_access`, `hearing_help`, `copies_questions_accessibility`, `other`. Route membership must be reciprocal and references valid; reject orphan/mixed-purpose membership, singleton groups and cross-event substitutions. Role compatibility is explicit: submission/submission; hearing_registration/hearing_registration; hearing_access/other; hearing_help and copies_questions_accessibility/information_contact; other/other. Unknown or uncertain functional binding remains explicit review, never role promotion.

Group evidence must cite the source context claiming alternatives, not merely endpoint quotations; source-resolved semantics remain a claim for independent verification. Preserve shared event scope and linked qualification requirements (e.g. hearing-access restrictions); grouping cannot erase route-specific requirements. Co-listed contacts alone do not establish interchangeability. Venue/in-person and oral/written presentation are different functional levels and must not become one certified group. One route represents one role/function; the same address serving different purposes may appear in distinct routes with the same exact components. Group IDs and component IDs are assessed by the stronger verifier as well as route IDs. Count groups and components against the unchanged 200 aggregate-item cap; exceeding it is an honest rejection, not an excuse to drop facts.

### Disclosures, ancillary facts and completeness

Preserve R6 requirement `semantic_class` and `modality`. Explicitly ask inventory and both verifier transports to distinguish public posting/publication/confidentiality scope and exceptions from submission prerequisites, and to retain consideration policies as separately typed disclosures where source-supported. Do not implement a keyword classifier or force informational modality solely from disclosure class. Record ancillary accessibility, copies, questions, technical help, helper links, office/address/phone relationships and their limitations with actual roles and associations, including document-level requirements. Supporting prose only inside an evidence quote is not an explicit recovered claim.

`coverage.status=claimed_complete` remains an extractor claim, not semantic proof. Empty arrays do not establish absence. Unknown/uncertain fields keep review reasons; favorable synthetic verifier replies cannot erase them. Missing/mistyped disclosures or ancillary endpoints may be structurally valid, but must fail the development semantic audit/completeness controls. No assembler promise of source completeness and no automatic repairs.

### Request admission and paired verifier compatibility

Retain source cap 30,000 codepoints, document/page/call/completion caps, model/provider settings, conservative byte estimates and existing budget ceilings. No limits or budget increases, truncation, chunking, compacting evidence to hide omissions, retries, provider fallback or paid tests. Perform offline admission on actual final model-specific request bodies, including final schema and prompt/probe wording. The exact duplicate schema relocation into response_format may remain; no other unreviewed transport reduction.

Expose deterministic admission results with stage, actual serialized body hash/bytes, estimate, applicable cap, admitted/rejected, reason and source hash. Inventory rejection routes to `request_size_review` before any operation intent. If either verifier request rejects, neither arm dispatches; retain valid record/common-payload hashes and name the failing stage. Source and every fact are preserved on admission or honestly rejected. Kentucky is allowed to remain rejected. Generate and compare the identical canonical full-source+record payload for Jev Choice probes and stronger generative verdict before any mock dispatch; preserve different provider response shapes and exact evidence validation. Offline receipts establish internal admission under pinned heuristics, not real provider acceptance/context limits.

### Observation and evaluator boundary

Define strict R7 observed schema and a single deterministic runtime-terminal-to-observed adapter. Each document requires `runtime_gate`, `gate_reasons`, inventory/record/payload hashes (nullable when absent), `provider_service_error`, and exactly `jev` and `stronger` arm entries. Each arm includes `status`, `called`, payload hash, source-coverage flag and reason. Pre-verifier source/size/assembly gates yield system review plus both arms `not_started`, `called:false`, coverage false; they are unserved, not provider failures. Preserve existing hashes when an assembled payload existed. Do not invent verifier output to satisfy schema.

Called judgments use existing accepted_complete/review/failed distinctions with paired payload/record identity required. An actual failed call is `failed`, `called:true`; a later undispatched arm is `not_started`, never an outage by implication. Reject accepted/review without a called arm and artifact hashes, unequal paired hashes, impossible gate/status combinations and favorable completeness with unresolved extraction review reasons. Missing observed rows remain unserved with an explicitly reported missing-observation count, not evidence that a gate was reached. Positive shadow acceptance still requires all five dimensions, no unresolved review reasons, and independent complete/correct source mapping for useful coverage; it never sets action_ready or production_eligible.

Evaluator preserves scheduled and all-collected denominators, independent source-unit and claim scoring, category/subtype/literal metrics, unknown-vs-zero costs and null zero-acceptance ratios. Add gate counts and called/uncalled counts separately from verdict outcomes. Report scheduled cohort size dynamically; keep the existing eight-notice/six-state adequacy policy explicitly separate from descriptive development cohorts. No R6 report regeneration/replacement is part of this work. The adapter and evaluator must agree directly without a postrun status-only rewrite.

## Interpretation and next decision

Passing controls demonstrates bounded representation, deterministic evidence preservation, internal request routing and runtime/evaluator compatibility. It does not demonstrate successful new model extraction, improved real recall, source fidelity, human-gold accuracy, provider admission, or comparative verifier performance. Development replay and authored migration remain explicitly separate from a future unseen validation cohort.

Any future live experiment needs a separate reviewed freeze, new explicit provider/budget authorization and unseen sources with independent source-first labels frozen before outputs, using the same whole-notice denominator and preregistered paired acceptance policy. The current work ends at offline verification.

Review decisions: approve the bounded component/group schema change and stricter aggregate accounting; confirm omission/classification controls belong to semantic audit rather than automatic assembly classification; accept honest large-request rejection. These are research contract choices, not a production architecture ADR or state-support expansion.
