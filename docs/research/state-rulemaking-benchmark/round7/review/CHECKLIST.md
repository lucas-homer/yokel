Status: prepared from frozen R6 code and retained evidence on 2026-10-09. R7 SPEC.md was not present at the initial review. This is a review gate, not approval of an unseen specification or implementation. Ownership is limited to round7/review/; no R6 artifacts, implementation, providers, credentials or production systems were changed.

- [Independent audit](../../round6/protocol/postrun-audit/README.md) and [method](../../round6/protocol/postrun-audit/METHOD.md): 82/120 source units recovered across the four collected notices, 82/89 among returned inventories; three ancillary omissions, four mismatches, three unsupported completeness claims. Kentucky's 31 source units remain unserved. Labels are independent AI reference, not human gold.
- [R6 result](../../round6/README.md): one request-size rejection, three assembly reviews, zero accepted records, no comparative verifier evidence; four notices do not fulfill the preregistered eight-notice cohort.
- Frozen [pipeline](../../round6/pipeline/pipeline.py): `VERSION=round6-participation-record-1.0.2`; `resolve_evidence` (line 110), `explicit_date` (157), `relationship_errors` (181), `assemble` (230), common payload and probes (311–332). [Interface](../../round6/pipeline/INTERFACE.md) preserves full source, exact selectors, all facts, typed uncertainty and separate semantic auditing.
- Frozen [provider adapters](../../round6/runtime/provider_adapters.py): `LIMITS` and `reserve` use conservative serialized-byte estimates plus allowance, not measured provider token limits. Frozen [runner](../../round6/runtime/run_pipeline.py) (126–146) gates extraction/assembly/request admission and verifies identical paired inner payloads. Frozen [evaluator](../../round6/protocol/evaluate.py) (105–112) cannot represent arm-level `review` without record/payload hashes; the R6 audit required an explicit status-only adapter for genuinely uncalled arms.

## Blocking criteria for spec approval

For each item, require a concrete representation or policy, an owner/consumer, and positive and negative acceptance controls. “Preserve correctness” without an executable boundary is insufficient.

| ID | Required contract and acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- |
| B1: frozen lineage | R7 has distinct pipeline/schema/prompt/runtime/evaluation versions and immutable input lineage. R6 raw sources, inventory responses, null records, outcomes, labels, denominator and hashes remain unchanged. Old versions are explicitly rejected or transformed by a named, separately retained development adapter; no implicit reinterpretation under an old version tag. |
| B2: exact evidence | Retain exact source quote/context/occurrence matching and deterministic Unicode offsets. Every component resolves to its own source evidence; absence, ambiguity, conflicting selectors, wrong document/hash and tampered assembly fail closed. No whitespace repair, fuzzy matching, generated punctuation in literal fields or source clipping. Quote resolution remains evidence integrity, not proof of role, applicability, truth or completeness. |
| B3: date policy | Declare accepted complete date grammar, weekday token grammar, and an explicit weekday/date consistency rule. Correct weekday prefixes admit the four observed Ohio dates; a mismatching weekday must be rejected or retained as explicit unresolved conflict without an accepted normalized date. No dropping the weekday to hide contradiction. Missing year, invalid calendar day, ambiguous numeric form, clock/timezone and relative timing never acquire inferred absolute date/time/UTC values. Preserve raw text and date-only precision. |
| B4: components/display | Define atomic source components, component IDs/types/evidence, route membership and optional display/description separately. A rendered multiline address/credential set must be traceable to supported components and a bounded deterministic rendering rule; added unsupported names, digits, endpoint or semantic connections fail. Descriptive activity language is not an exact literal. No unchecked freeform display escape hatch and no promotion of a display value into a filing destination. Exact spans alone do not establish that separated components belong together. |
| B5: alternatives/applicability | Define purpose and allowed role-purpose combinations, explicit group objects/members/references, relation evidence, event scope and applicability/conditions. Hearing access, hearing help, copies/questions/accessibility remain distinct from submission. Source list membership or shared contact identity is not proof of interchangeability. Preserve mixed-level or uncertain relations as uncertain; do not force venue/in-person and oral-presentation modalities into an accepted equivalent-alternative group. A dial-in limitation remains bound to its route. |
| B6: disclosure/completeness | Keep semantic disclosure typing distinct from instruction modality and from source text preservation. Include scope/exceptions and event/route binding. Full-source inventory and both verifier prompts must include ancillary offices, postal endpoints, helper links and public posting/publication/confidentiality disclosures. Evidence-only text does not count as recovered structured content. Exact phone/email/date values and valid assembly cannot establish completeness. |
| B7: size routing | Choose an explicit overlimit route before implementation: a retained fail-closed research outcome is valid. No silent source/fact/schema clipping, limit increase, weakening reservation, or invented provider-limit claim. Any lossless transport optimization must prove semantic equivalence and measure final serialized inventory and BOTH verifier requests, including prompts/schema/probes/escaping/allowance, against unchanged caps. If chunking is proposed, specify cross-chunk provenance, all-source completeness and relation reconciliation before approval. No new provider calls in this work. |
| B8: end-to-end compatibility | Specify new nested IDs in item enumeration, references, record schemas, common payload, Jev probes, stronger verdict coverage and independent evaluator matching. Every new relation/component must be audited exactly once or through an explicitly defined parent claim; no silent disappearance. Both arms receive equal canonical source+record evidence and compatible full semantic dimensions while retaining their distinct response capabilities. Payload/version/hash tampering fails closed. |
| B9: actual arm state | System gate state and each arm's invocation/judgment state are separate. Source/request/assembly rejection before verification yields truly uncalled arms with no invented verdict or provider failure. Distinguish request rejection after assembly (record/payload may exist), one arm called and the other uncalled, and a called arm with an error. Runtime terminal artifacts must feed evaluator without fake hashes, status promotion or undocumented repair. |
| B10: acceptance/reporting | Structural admission, semantic source correctness, omissions, model verdict and end-to-end useful complete acceptance remain distinct. Preserve conservative unresolved-state guards and action_ready/production_eligible false. Retain all scheduled notices and absent artifacts in denominators; zero acceptances yields null unsafe-acceptance fraction and null cost per correct complete accepted record. Counts must use actual cohort size, with unmet preregistered targets explicit. |
| B11: development/future evaluation | R6 replays, repaired inventories, hand-authored fixtures and old sources are development regressions only. Hash and label them as such. No training-on-test or repaired-R6 accuracy claims. Freeze a new reviewed pipeline/runtime/evaluator and independently collected pre-output reference before a future fresh bounded cohort; future collection/live execution is a separate authorized gate. Offline mocked requests demonstrate local admission/reachability, not actual provider acceptance, extraction recall or verifier quality. |

## Minimum positive and negative controls

These controls belong to implementation verification; this checklist does not claim they have run.

1. **Weekday and precision:** admit Monday July 13, 2026; Friday July 10, 2026; Monday October 5, 2026; Friday October 2, 2026. Reject or explicitly quarantine a Tuesday July 13, 2026 conflict. Include missing year, impossible date/leap day, unknown weekday spelling, unsupported trailing material, ambiguous numeric date and timezone-free clock. Preserve existing relative/conditional bindings without date arithmetic.
2. **Component provenance:** multiline address and Teams name/meeting ID/passcode resolve separately and render deterministically. Reject changed digits, unsupported separator text that introduces meaning, swapped component ownership, dangling/duplicate IDs, repeated quote without disambiguation, conflicting occurrence/context and correct literals from an unrelated event. Preserve exact activity literal `orally` separately from a description.
3. **Alternatives:** admit explicit phone “or via email” hearing-help alternatives with original role/purpose. Reject or flag unsupported grouping of merely adjacent contacts, purpose/role mismatch, cross-event group mixing and fabricated condition-free dial-in equivalence. Preserve the uncertain mixed-level hearing-mode relation without manufacturing certainty.
4. **Semantic negatives that can remain structurally valid:** correct text typed `other` instead of disclosure; omitted general-office address; omitted local-number helper link; omitted publisher office with phone retained; false claimed_complete; source-supported endpoint attached to the wrong purpose; lost applicability or confidentiality exception. Independent evaluation must score these as incorrect, incomplete or unknown as appropriate even when all exact spans resolve and verifiers are mocked favorable.
5. **Full-record behavior:** corrected development controls address all interacting failures, not only one category; original frozen inventories still reproduce their historical outcomes under R6. An invalid ancillary component cannot silently vanish to make the record valid. If partial diagnostic artifacts are introduced, they must be explicitly nonaccepted and cannot stand in for whole-record paired evidence.
6. **Request boundary:** entire large KY source and final schema/prompt wrappers; multibyte/escaped characters; exactly-at-limit and above-limit requests; larger normalized component/group record; Jev total and state-plus-longest-probe limits; stronger input/output reservation. Prove no source/fact loss and no transport call on local rejection; budget/reservation/call caps unchanged. Retain the rejection if it does not fit.
7. **Runtime/evaluator states:** no inventory after size rejection; inventory but no record after assembly rejection; record/payload exists but both requests uncalled after admission rejection; Jev response exists but stronger uncalled; one called arm fails; both called. Verify hash presence only for real artifacts, truthful invocation state and preserved denominator; never infer “both ran” from equal outcomes.
8. **Version/verdict compatibility:** R6 inventory does not silently pass as R7; supported migration has explicit provenance and development designation. New component/group IDs are covered by stronger verdict validation and semantic matching. Omitted/duplicate verdict assessment, mismatched payload, caller-mutated assembly and favorable verdict over explicit unresolved extraction fail conservative acceptance.
