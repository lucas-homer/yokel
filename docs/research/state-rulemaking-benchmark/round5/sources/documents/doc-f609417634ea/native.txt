                                                    Notice of Intent
    In accordance with the provisions of Section 4-168(a) of the Connecticut General Statutes (CGS) the
Criminal Justice Commission hereby gives notice that it proposes to adopt a regulation concerning
Criminal Justice Regulations.

Comment period (See CGS section 4-168(a)(1)(A))
     A comment period of not less than thirty days shall begin upon posting of this notice to the eRegulations System
     website and shall end on February 9, 2026.
Detailed description of the proposed regulation. (See CGS Sec. 4-168(a)(1)(B))

      The proposed regulation clarifies the Commission’s responsibilities concerning the appointment, discipline, and
      removal of prosecutors employed by the Division of Criminal Justice; adds language regarding the Office of
      Inspector General; addresses conflicts of interest involving the Inspector General; corrects the Criminal Justice
      Commission’s address of record; omits outdated language; and makes technical and other changes to reflect
      current law and practice.
Statement of the purposes for which the regulation is proposed. (See CGS Sec. 4-168(a)(1)(C))

     The purpose of the proposed regulation is to update the Criminal Justice Commission’s existing regulations,
     which were last amended in 1993.
Statutory authority (See CGS Sec. 4-168(a)(1)(D))

     C.G.S. Sec. 51-275a(g).
When, where and how to obtain a copy of the small business impact statement and, if applicable, the
regulatory flexibility analysis required pursuant to Sec. 4-168a. (See CGS Sec. 4-168(a)(1)(E))

     A copy of the proposed regulation, the Fiscal Note, and the Regulatory Flexibility Analysis applicable to the
     proposed regulation shall be available for inspection at the Office of the Chief State's Attorney, 300 Corporate
     Place, Rocky Hill, CT 06067 Attn: Marisa DePhillips, by requesting a copy of the same by telephoning the Office
     of the Chief State's Attorney at (860)258-5800, or by viewing the same by visiting the e-Regulations portal on the
     website of the Secretary of the State: http://eregulations.ct.gov/eRegsPortal/Browse/ProposedRegulations.
When, where and how interested persons may present their views on the proposed regulation
(See CGS Sec. 4-168(a)(1)(F))


     All interested parties who wish to submit comments, data, views, or arguments may do so in writing.
     Pursuant to C.G.S. Sec. 4-168(b), the Agency shall hold a public hearing on the proposed regulation if
     requested by fifteen persons, by a governmental subdivision or agency, or by an association having not less
     than fifteen members, if notice of the request is received by the agency not later than fourteen days after the
     date of posting of the notice by the agency on the eRegulations System. The public comment shall be
     extended upon posting a copy of the proposed regulation to ensure all interested persons shall have a
     reasonable opportunity to submit their views.

     Such written materials should be directed to Kathryn Bare, Executive Assistant State's Attorney, Office of the
     Chief State's Attorney, and sent via:
