Full notice text reaches semantic verification within fixed limits; oversize or incomplete input reviews without truncation. Follow `CONTRACT-GUIDANCE.md`: exact evidence/calendar/email checks remain deterministic, while the frozen verifier distinguishes incidental wording from conditions actually binding a candidate.
