# Round 6 primary scope — design phase

User authorized the proposed next comparison on October 7, 2026. Research-only, no production/schema/deployment changes. Prior rounds immutable. Separate existing visible Codex child tasks are reused.

The objective is native source text to a complete evidence-backed participation record, with identical records independently evaluated by Jev and a stronger verifier. Measure omissions as well as unsupported claims. Source-first requirement inventory must be generated without reviewer labels or selected candidate answers; verifier also sees original full native source to catch omissions from the inventory. No automatic claim that extraction and verification are statistically independent, especially if sharing a model family.

Execution sequence: shared representation/evaluation contract; pipeline/runtime development using old sources and synthetic regressions; independent review; freeze implementation/prompts/selection; acquire eight fresh complete notices from at least six states and at most40pages; freeze source; independent image-only labels; freeze labels; bounded live extraction and paired verification; source-grounded scoring and final report. No fresh sources or calls until released by primary. Do not create overlapping alternative schemas/frameworks.

Approved limits: eight sources, six general plus two natural conditional/negative controls, max two per state, max40pages; original native text cap30000codepoints. Conservative total experiment cap $2; maximum eight inventory, eight batched Jev and eight stronger-verifier POSTs (24 total). OpenRouter GPT-6.1 Sol at low effort, regular OpenAI only, no fallback; conservative input35000, completion6144 inventory/4096 stronger. Jev1.13.0 conservative total64000 and state+longest32000. Nominal worst-case reservation$1.960704.  no OCR, no automatic POST retries, no silent model fallback or context clipping. Runtime owner must prove the max reservation fits before freeze; adjust design/caps, not post-hoc budget. Credential reads only through existing authorized1Password refs, runtime only, never output keys. Primary executes provider calls.

A complete record needs source identity/provenance and unresolved identity discrepancies; typed participation events/phases; dates and their precision/relative/conditional status; alternative submission routes; exact required references/formats; applicable timing/other instructions; public-disclosure statements separately typed; exact source evidence and relationships. Absence/uncertainty must not collapse into a confidently empty set. Dates and addresses alone do not establish record completeness. Distinct phases/alternative destinations are not inherently conflicts.

Evaluate scalar literal correctness, relationship/requirement preservation, source-coverage omissions, complete-record acceptance, unsafe acceptance, review fraction and failure counts, costs by extraction and verifier arm. Fresh notices are the unit; field/requirement counts descriptive and clustered within notices. No claims of representative national accuracy. Assess final records against independently frozen image truth, not just the source inventory produced by the extractor. All scheduled inputs and service failures remain in denominators. Use this pilot for measurable architecture choices, not production certification.
