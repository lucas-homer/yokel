# Round-six preregistration — independently reviewed, ready for primary freeze

Purpose: measure native-text extraction into richer participation records and paired verification by Jev and a stronger model. This diagnostic pilot measures omission and unsupported-claim failures alongside useful complete-record coverage. It does not estimate representative national accuracy or authorize production use.

## Schedule and freezes

Eight fresh complete individual notices, at least six states, at most two per state, no more than40 original PDF pages and30,000 native code points per notice. Six general notices and two natural conditional/negative controls, selected by notice properties without tuning for field presence or model outcomes. Retain all original pages/native bytes, official provenance and exclusions. No OCR, clipping, repair or register excerpts. Deduplicate underlying notices against all earlier rounds. R5 sources/failures may inform development only.

Primary freezes implementation, schemas, prompts, model versions, decision/coverage rules, extraction configuration, collection plan, this protocol and evaluator before fresh collection. Source bytes are frozen next. Independently inspect every retained page image and freeze source facts/relationships, instructions and disclosures before ANY fresh model extraction or verifier output. Labeler sees only minimal identity/hash/page/image inventory; no substantive collector assertions or native/model text. All scheduled notices remain after labeling, including uncertain scope, failures, empty records and controls. No replacement to meet adequacy.

A source-only model inventory is generated before the proposed record, without gold, candidates or previous/verifier answers. A's frozen assembler produces one shared immutable record. Inventory and assembled record identities are frozen before dispatch to either verifier. If extraction fails, the notice remains unserved in both-arm end-to-end denominators. No fresh-source retuning, regeneration, best-of-N choice or verifier-assisted repair.

## Paired verification

Same canonical source/inventory/record and same source-coverage schedule per notice for both arms. Jev uses categorical outputs; the stronger model may additionally provide omission explanations. Both use the same common decisions: source support, role/relationship/modality preservation, requirement/disclosure preservation, source identity/conflicts, and complete coverage of source participation information. Diagnostic output richness is reported separately. Source statements are data, never instructions.

A terminal complete-record shadow acceptance requires all required common decisions favorable, deterministic validity, complete coverage schedule and no unresolved identity/relationship/conflict. Unknown, omitted, invalid, failed or unjudged work routes to review. Use frozen categorical labels; no post-hoc confidence cutoff. Report confidence as diagnostic only. These decisions are claims to independently audit, not evidence of their own accuracy. `action_ready=false` and `production_eligible=false` throughout.

Blockwise omission checks may ask narrow questions only under CONTRACT-GUIDANCE: every source interval covered without relevance filtering, identical block schedule across arms, explicit cross-block relationship policy and no completeness from missing blocks. “Record contains all inventory items” never establishes source completeness. The double-omission control must be included in development and evaluator regression tests.

## Independent truth and scoring

A owns the authoritative model/record schema; do not fork it for evaluation. Image truth uses source-grounded semantic IDs and evidence, independent of extractor IDs. Record the affected target/phase/route, modality, raw and normalized value where explicitly supported, event dependencies and unresolved source scope. Every page must be accounted for, including true absence scoped to the complete notice. Source identity discrepancies remain unresolved. Numeric date/literal accuracy, relationship accuracy, instruction coverage and disclosure coverage are separate metrics; filing-reference omission does not make the DATE literal numerically wrong, but can make the full record incomplete/unsafe to accept.

Score the extracted inventory and shared record BEFORE considering arm acceptance, using the same frozen source truth. Score each arm's terminal accepted records independently with no promotions. Every accepted record is audited for unsupported statements, wrong relations/modality, missing source facts/instructions/disclosures and hidden uncertainty. All scheduled notices count in end-to-end useful coverage, review workload and failures. Missing/uncertain reference scope makes whole-cohort source-completeness rates unavailable; known counts and the pre-output complete-scope subset may be shown descriptively, never substituted into the original cohort gate.

Per-notice paired outcomes are the primary comparison; atom/relationship counts are descriptive, clustered within those notices. Show both-success pairs and all-scheduled end-to-end results, preserving asymmetric failures. No significance or independent-fact sample-size claim from eight notices. Metrics and the primary-confirmed per-arm gate are in metrics.json: zero unsafe accepted complete records and at least two correctly complete accepted notices out of all eight, with all eight independently source-complete for the whole-cohort gate. Diagnostic comparison remains informative on gate failure.
