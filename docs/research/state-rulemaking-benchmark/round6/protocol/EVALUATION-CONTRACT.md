# Round6 independent truth, matching audit and scoring contract

This is an evaluator-only boundary, not a replacement for A's record schema. Model source/inventory/record formats remain A's. The evaluator is a small stdlib program; it neither calls a model nor parses model responses into truth. Its inputs are the frozen schedule, pre-extraction image truth, independently audited source/record matching, and observed terminal states/costs. A model's confidence, completeness claim or omission explanation never supplies a scoring verdict.

## Fixed completeness universe

Include the notice's participation identity, events/phases and timing (including hearing and hearing signup/request); public participation submission/registration routes and alternatives; applicability relationships; instructions, required references/formats and modality; disclosures about participation/public posting/confidentiality; and source uncertainty/conflict/lifecycle that affects those facts. Clock/relative expressions stay source-grounded; no inferred date arithmetic. Group repeated semantically identical mentions into one source unit before outputs, retaining all image evidence. Same value in different phases, roles or material conditions is a different unit.

Hearing signup is participation: retain its event, cutoff/prerequisite and route with the actual signup role, not written-comment delivery. Accessibility, copy-request and query contacts are ancillary: label each distinct explicit notice contact/function as `ancillary` with its literal(s), role and relevant participation limitation. Full record may retain that role or explicitly annotate it out of the direct-submission target. Either representation can satisfy the source unit if semantically faithful. Count ancillary coverage separately; do not add them to direct-route recall or silently discard them from completeness. A notice with only copies contact cannot become a supported comment route. Repeated contact for both copies and comments preserves both roles; corresponding source units/relations remain distinct.

Exclude the substantive proposed rule's program obligations, benefit/application deadlines, ordinary legal citations, descriptive policy analysis and routine publication metadata that do not identify or qualify participation. Necessary docket/proceeding identity and operative publication anchors remain included. Pre-extraction labels retain an exclusion rationale for substantive sections so model output cannot change the denominator. Do not enumerate every irrelevant citation as a required record fact. Unexplained empty inventories never establish absence.

Seven image-truth categories: `fact`, `relationship`, `instruction`, `disclosure`, `ancillary`, `identity`, `uncertainty`. Facts carry subtypes such as phase, timing, direct_route or signup_route; instructions retain mandatory/optional/conditional/uncertain modality and affected action; disclosure is separately typed. Relationships connect source-unit IDs or `$notice`. Distinct source units are the denominator; no automatic matching by equal date/email or model IDs.

## Image truth before extraction

`truth.json` uses `round6.truth.v1`: documents contain `document_id`, `pdf_sha256`, `scope_status` (complete/incomplete/uncertain), `all_pages_viewed`, `universe_complete`, `domains` (one present/absent/uncertain status for all seven categories), `units`, `acceptance_blockers`, `exclusions`, reviewer and review evidence. Every unit has `id`, `category`, `subtype`, `description`, `raw_value` (nullable), `modality`, `targets` (source IDs or `$notice`), `evidence` (physical page/region/exact quote), and optional explicitly supported normalized value. Absence needs full-notice review; empty category is not inferred from a model. Scope/identity uncertainty is recorded before outputs, never corrected to hit the eight-notice quota.

`acceptance_blockers` lists source uncertainty units that prohibit a complete-record acceptance even if faithfully disclosed: unresolved notice identity, competing/unresolved controlling scope or missing source continuation. An explicit conditional/hearing-bound event is not automatically such a blocker: its conditional relationship can be faithfully represented without implying an unconditional deadline. Record semantic completeness and acceptance eligibility remain separate.

Every required source category/domain must be adjudicated and every viewed page recorded in a separate image ledger. Truth schema plus inventory/page hashes and a READY manifest are frozen before first extraction. Evaluator checks label coverage; primary retains timing/provenance receipts. Neither this evaluator nor a self-attested boolean proves temporal blinding.

## Postrun semantic matching audit

`matching-audit.json` uses `round6.matching-audit.v1`, one document per schedule. For each available `inventory` and `record`, retain its exact `sha256` and an independently reviewed assessment:

- `review_complete`: all semantic claims and relationships in this artifact have been reviewed, including extras.
- `claims`: each independently enumerated claim has `id`, `categories`, `status` (correct/incorrect/unknown), `literal_kind` (date/email/other/none), `literal_status` (correct/incorrect/unknown/not_applicable), `evidence_status` (matched/invalid/unknown), and `basis`. Literal correctness is separate from full semantic status: correct DATE plus wrong phase can be literal-correct/semantically-incorrect.
- `source_matches`: exactly one row per frozen source unit, with `source_unit_id`, `status` (matched/omitted/mismatched/unknown), `claim_ids` and `basis`. A match requires semantic value/type, role, modality, applicability and evidence preservation, not string equality. Missing source atoms are omitted even if absent from BOTH inventory and record. A relation cannot be matched unless its required endpoint units are matched. A single rich claim may represent several source units; no source unit is counted twice.

The reviewer maps claims from A's structure and literal source evidence; the evaluator does not infer these mappings or accept a verifier's self-assessment as an audit. Every claim must be listed even when unsupported or outside the intended target. Pure correctly marked background may be categorized as ancillary/context for precision without creating a new required source fact; label `status=correct` only when its out-of-target role is explicit and supported. Any incorrect/unknown/evidence-invalid extra prevents complete support. If the artifact does not exist use null assessment; it gets no recovery, not an audited empty set.

A complete assessment must account for every truth unit and have nonempty claims for matched/mismatched units, none for omitted units; references and IDs are checked. The evaluator demands exact observed artifact hashes. It refuses inconsistent audits rather than guessing or silently dropping rows. An accepted but unaudited record is unsafe and gets no coverage credit. All six R5 weaknesses are development material only; no prior labels are changed.

## Frozen schedule

`schedule.json` uses `round6.schedule.v1`, with documents containing unique `document_id`, exact `pdf_sha256`, positive physical `pdf_pages`, state, and stratum (`general` or `control`). Cohort adequacy requires eight notices, six general/two controls, at least six states, no more than two notices per state and at most40 pages. The source pipeline independently enforces native-codepoint and freshness limits.

## Observed execution input

`observed.json` uses `round6.observed.v1`, one document per scheduled input with `inventory_sha256`, `record_sha256` (nullable when absent), `payload_sha256` (nullable until verification dispatch), and exactly two arms named `jev` and `stronger`. Each arm has `status`: accepted_complete, review, failed or not_started; `payload_sha256` (same shared canonical payload for dispatched results), `source_coverage_complete`, and `failure_reason` when applicable. These are independently verified from retained runtime artifacts; no model-generated audit. Missing scheduled observed entry becomes not_started for both arms. Extra/duplicate schedule/observation IDs are rejected. Shared payload hash mismatch is an invalid comparison, not a favorable per-arm result.

Costs are separate `costs` entries for extraction/jev/stronger, with `usage_estimate_usd`, `conservative_charge_usd`, `provider_reported_usd` (each nullable when unknown). They cover all intents including failed/uncertain calls, under primary's durable ledger. Never treat missing cost as zero. Total experiment cost sums shared extraction once and both arms; arm scenario cost is shared extraction plus that arm. All ratios are null for zero correct-complete accepted records. Primary's operational budget remains enforced by runtime, not this evaluator.

## Deterministic score and confirmed gate

Given these independent audit inputs, scoring is deterministic. Report inventory and record coverage and claim precision by category, plus coverage by source-fact subtype; scalar date/email accuracy; claim correctness/unsupported-or-unknown additions; per-notice record completeness and acceptance blockers. All scheduled known source units remain denominators, including failed extraction. Whole-reference rates are null if any source record lacks complete scope/all-pages/universe review, or an existing artifact lacks a complete independent matching audit; known counts and source-complete subset are descriptive. Conditional controls may be complete when correctly represented. No blank/zero-denominator category becomes100%.

Per arm report accepted, correctly complete accepted, unsafe accepted, semantic review, service failure/not_started, review-or-unserved fraction and missing source-coverage checks. Zero accepted makes unsafe fraction null and provides no safety evidence. Useful coverage requires a source-complete correctly audited record, a full observed coverage pass and accepted_complete, divided by all eight scheduled notices. Primary-confirmed gate: schedule/cohort adequacy, all eight source-complete, zero unsafe accepted records and at least2/8 correctly complete accepted. Per-notice paired outcomes preserve asymmetric failures; never take a best-of-two union. Costs and issue flags do not retroactively alter semantic labels. Human review time remains unmeasured.

## Final runtime-to-audit boundary

The final runtime's `complete_record_shadow_accepted=true` with terminal `status=text_supported` maps to evaluator `accepted_complete`; semantic or structural review maps to `review`. Use retained operation stop/intent and the full frozen schedule to distinguish `failed` from `not_started`, including documents interrupted by a global stop. An existing completed arm may be reconstructed from its immutable raw result and frozen decision rule when the other arm stopped before the paired terminal was written; document that reconstruction and never substitute an unobserved verifier answer. No promotion from a partial response is allowed.

`source_coverage_complete` records completion of the frozen all-source question/assessment schedule over the exact shared payload, not source truth or agreement with the gold. Jev must return all five questions; stronger output must pass its full item-coverage/schema checks. Rehash actual inventory and record bytes used for each audit consistently and retain the exact serialization. Source-only inventory and normalized record are scored separately. The independent matching audit, not runtime verdict confidence, establishes semantic correctness.
