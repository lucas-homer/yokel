                      WITHDRAWAL OF PROPOSED RULES
                         DEPARTMENT OF COMMERCE
                     (By the Code Reviser's Office)
                         [Filed February 23, 2026, 10:23 a.m.]

WAC 365-196-450 and 365-196-480, proposed by the department of com-
merce in WSR 25-13-090, appearing in issue 25-13 of the Washington
State Register, which was distributed on July 2, 2025, is withdrawn by
the office of the code reviser under RCW 34.05.335(3), since the pro-
posal was not adopted within the 180-day period allowed by the stat-
ute.
