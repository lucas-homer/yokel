                    PREPROPOSAL STATEMENT OF INQUIRY
                          BOARD OF REGISTRATION
                       FOR PROFESSIONAL ENGINEERS
                            AND LAND SURVEYORS
                         [Filed April 1, 2026, 11:20 a.m.]

     Subject of Possible Rule Making: Chapter 196-21 WAC, Land survey-
ors-in-training.
     Statutes Authorizing the Agency to Adopt Rules on this Subject:
RCW 18.43.035.
     Reasons Why Rules on this Subject may be Needed and What They
Might Accomplish: Changes are necessary to implement chapter 134, Laws
of 2026 (chapter 18.43 RCW). Changes will be made to reflect changes
in licensing/certification processes, and clarify licensing require-
ments.
     Process for Developing New Rule: Notice will be made to stake-
holders and active licensees via the board of registration for profes-
sional engineers and land surveyors' (board) contact lists, and will
be posted on our website and distributed through the board and board
staff. Interested parties may contact the individual listed below and
the public may also participate by commenting after amendments are
proposed by providing written comments or giving oral testimony during
the public hearing process.
     Interested parties can participate in the decision to adopt the
new rule and formulation of the proposed rule before publication by
contacting Shanan Gillespie, P.O. Box 9025, Olympia, WA 98507-9025,
phone 360-664-1570, TTY 711, email shanan.gillespie@brpels.wa.gov,
website https://brpels.wa.gov/about-us/laws-and-rules/rulemaking-
activity.
