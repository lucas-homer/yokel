SECOND NOTICE OF MODIFICATIONS TO TEXT OF PROPOSED REGULATIONS  
AND ADDITION OF DOCUMENTS TO RULEMAKING FILE 
Pursuant to the requirements of Government Code section 11346.8(c), and 
section 44 of Title 1 of the California Code of Regulations, the Department of 
Motor Vehicles is providing a second notice of changes made to proposed 
regulations originally noticed to the public in the California Regulatory Notice 
Register on October 31, 2025, related to the Electronic Lien and Titling Program. 
A Notice of Modification was also provided to the public on April 23, 2026.   
Additionally, the department is providing notice that documents have been 
added to the rulemaking file and are available to the public for inspection and 
comment. The documents added to the rulemaking file are as follows:  
• Second Modified Regulatory Text 
• Statement of Reasons for the Second Modified Regulatory Text 
• Revised ELT Program Service Provider Application, form REG 670 (Rev. 
8/2026), incorporated by reference in Section 153.04 
• Revised ELT Program Lienholder Application, form REG 671 (Rev. 8/2026), 
incorporated by reference in Section 153.22 
If you have any comments regarding the proposed changes or the documents 
and other information, the department will accept written comments between 
October 5, 2026 through October 20, 2026. All written comments must be 
submitted to the department no later than October 20, 2026, and addressed to: 
Randi Calkins, Regulations Analyst 
Department of Motor Vehicles 
Legal Affairs Division 
2415 First Avenue, MS C-128 
Sacramento, CA 95818 
Email: LADRegulations@dmv.ca.gov 
All written comments received by October 
20, 2026, which pertain to the 
indicated changes or the above-listed documents and other information, will be 
reviewed and any objections and/or recommendations responded to by the 
department’s staff in the Final Statement of Reasons as part of the compilation 
of the rulemaking file.
