NOTICE OF PROPOSED REGULATIONS
Invasive Mussels [Revision]
September 2, 2026
15-DAY CONTINUATION OF NOTICE IS HEREBY GIVEN that the Department proposes 
amendments to sections 650, 672, 672.1, and 672.2 to Title 14, California Code of Regulations 
(CCR), for Invasive Mussels.
The full text of the resulting amendment with the change clearly indicated, shall be made 
available to the public for at least 15 days before the agency amends, or repeals the 
resulting regulation. Any written comments received regarding the change will be
responded to in the final statement of reasons required by Section 11346.9.
On July 3, 2026, a notice of proposed action (NOPA) to adopt the above referenced 
regulations was distributed and noticed in compliance with Government Code section 
11346.4. The NOPA stated that the Department was proposing to amend sections 650, 672, 
672.1, and 672.2 to Title 14 of the CCR to expand the scope of the existing regulations to 
encompass all invasive mussel species, thereby ensuring consistency with the revised Fish 
and Game Code 2301, 2302, and new Fish and Game Code 2303 established by Assembly 
Bill 149. The proposed amendments incorporate components of prevention and 
containment to limit the spread of invasive mussels in California and to other 
states. Additionally, the proposal amends the Invasive Mussel Permit Application Form (DFW 
1014), the Quarantine Notice Form (DFW 1015), the Administrative Penalty Form (DFW 1016), 
and creates a new Department Business List Request Form (DFW 1013). 
The comment period for the proposed regulation ended on August 18, 2026. Consideration 
of input received resulted in modification to the originally proposed text in the paragraph of 
subsection 672.1(c)(1) to clarify who may conduct inspections of conveyances, and is 
provided with this notice in accordance with Government Code section 11346.8(c) and Title 
1, Section 44 CCR. 
The regulation with the proposed changes indicated in double underline (for added text) 
and single strikeout (for removed text) is attached to this notice.
