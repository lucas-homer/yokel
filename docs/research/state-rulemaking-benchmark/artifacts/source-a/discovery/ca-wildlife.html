Interested persons may submit a request for electronic notices of proposed Department regulatory actions by sending an email to Regulations@wildlife.ca.gov, where you will be placed on a mailing list to receive such notices when they are available. Please note: these are separate from those under Fish and Game Commission rulemaking authority. For proposed Fish and Game Commission regulations, visit fgc.ca.gov/Regulations (opens in new tab).
