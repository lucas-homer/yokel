# Fresh evaluation of date and email extraction

Evaluate an actual automated pipeline on 12 previously unseen full notices from at least six states. The pipeline uses native text first, deterministic candidate and context selection, Jev semantic judgments and an OCR fallback when its frozen policy requires it. The only target fields are an explicit absolute written-comment close **date** and a direct written-comment submission **email**. Outputs are research shadow observations. This evaluation authorizes no production ingestion, message submission, deadline calculation or use of an unverified destination.

## Scope and source selection

Collection begins only after the primary freezes the complete implementation, prompts, rules, routing and resource limits. The collector must not have researched or inspected these candidate notices for this round before that freeze. None of the prior 28 notices, their original PDFs, or previously used near-duplicate notices may enter the fresh set. Select new publication/template families where feasible and report any repeat family explicitly; do not describe familiar-template performance as transfer to new families.

Retain 12 complete notices from at least six states, aiming for four genuine scanned or degraded publisher originals. Do not rasterize a clean PDF to manufacture a scan. Keep original bytes and an official retrieval URL, retrieval time, SHA-256, original page count and the basis for the scan/degradation classification. Distinguish image-only, mixed-image/text, visibly degraded and clean born-digital inputs. A scan has source-image evidence, not merely a low native-text count. Use bounded full notices compatible with the 60-page OCR ceiling; do not remove notice pages or substantive attachments just to fit. If a complete notice is extracted from a register, preserve the original and exact physical-page map, document that collection supplied the notice boundary, and do not claim whole-register discovery or segmentation performance.

Freeze the selection ledger and inaccessible/rejected candidates with reasons. Selection may balance states and scan strata using source properties, never pipeline outcomes. Source-only review may identify scope shortcomings before the source freeze; after it, do not replace difficult, unsupported, inaccessible or failed notices. Retain quota shortfalls and all scheduled notices. Fewer than 12 notices or six states is an unmet evaluation requirement; a scan target shortfall limits any scan conclusion. Do not quietly substitute fake scans or expand the budget.

## Two freezes and the handoff

Freeze A covers the source-input interface, extraction and candidate code, deterministic rules, routing/merge policy, model IDs, prompts, serializer, this protocol and caps. The offline evaluator scaffold may be finalized separately during collection, as authorized by the primary, but its schema and scoring code must freeze before source labeling and any runtime/model outputs. This does not permit outcome-tuned scoring. Every owner first writes its final artifacts, verifies hashes, then declares READY with an exact manifest hash. After READY that owner stops editing those artifacts. The primary records Freeze A only after all acknowledgments and verifies those same hashes. Only then may collection start.

The collector renders all pages directly from original/retained PDF bytes. The independent reviewer labels from page images and official source provenance, without seeing native parser text, OCR/model output, runtime candidates or decisions. Mechanical extraction metadata may be prepared, but it is not reference evidence. Prefer postponing all candidate-producing native extraction until Freeze B. If any native preparation already occurred, record exactly when and whether any labeler saw it; do not call it fully output-blind. Freeze B binds all source PDFs/images, page maps, independent labels and the runtime metadata manifest before pipeline execution.

A label owner follows the same READY acknowledgment rule before Freeze B. New ambiguity after either freeze is a new version or append-only deviation; do not edit frozen files, overwrite historical manifests or retrospectively relabel successes. If code must change after fresh sources are exposed, retain the failure and old results; the revised code has consumed development evidence and needs a later fresh evaluation. A clerical source-label correction must preserve initial and corrected results and be applied symmetrically without model-dependent choices.

## Runtime input and isolation

Use the pipeline's published SOURCE-INPUT-CONTRACT.md. Runtime receives only opaque document IDs, complete PDF/native text bytes, hashes, mechanical page metadata and declared retained scope. Evaluator labels, gold dates/addresses, hand-selected decisive snippets, known expected failure modes and prior case IDs remain in a separate evaluation bundle. Validate an allowlist and exact hashes before reading inputs. Stage only allowed data and code; report whether filesystem read isolation is enforced or merely a code/data separation convention.

All candidate finding and context selection must be automated. Every emitted Jev case includes the full retained notice text within the frozen limits; local candidate spans locate evidence, not reviewer-selected replacement context. A document exceeding context or candidate limits abstains without truncation or silently dropping candidates. Distinguish an automatically selected paragraph from a proven complete notice. Empty extraction, page mismatch, unknown/slice scope, missing candidate coverage signals or ambiguous associations trigger the frozen fallback/review policy.

Runtime cannot inspect fallback output before requesting it. Pin the native extractor, OCR access path/engine, exact annotation serialization, Jev version, fixed four-dimensional criteria and routing/merge rules. One fallback attempt per notice; no source rewriting or model repair of retained strings. Conflicting native/OCR values for the same role force review of that role unless the pre-frozen code has an objective provenance rule; agreement is corroboration, not proof of PDF fidelity. Other independently supported fields may remain partial observations, never whole-notice readiness.

## Dates and destinations

Code owns literal copying, exact evidence-span checks, date validation and normalization. Accepted dates require an explicit supported year, month and day, in a format covered by the frozen grammar. Invalid dates fail. Ambiguous numeric formats, missing components, relative deadlines, conditions outside the supported grammar, and unresolved competing deadlines abstain. Do not calculate business days, import a year from another event, infer a clock/timezone, or turn an anticipated date into an actual deadline. Keep date-only precision even when the source also contains an unmodeled clock; this experiment does not certify exact cutoff time. Preserve any observed time/zone in raw evidence without making a normalized claim about it.

Code compares proposed submission emails literally with the extracted candidate span, including the local part. Do not correct spelling, strip characters or infer an address. Any normalization for comparison is reversible and preserves the raw string; domain case-folding does not license local-part rewriting. The semantic question establishes submission role, not delivery validity. Staff/contact addresses are not automatically comment destinations. No email or network delivery test is part of this experiment.

A corrupted parser email can still pass exact copying and semantic support checks. Independently score whether it matches the original PDF. Jev cannot verify an unseen image or recover an omitted letter through confidence. A well-formed date can likewise be wrong to the source. Keep source fidelity distinct from support in the available text.

## Acceptance and unsupported cases

Use the fixed four-label gate inherited from the reviewed verifier: support=supported, role=matched, condition=preserved and conflict=absent, with all deterministic and operation checks passing. Confidence/probabilities remain diagnostics. No threshold optimization on fresh outputs. The pipeline may deliberately exclude conditional and relative scopes even when a model can interpret them; those cases must remain explicit unsupported abstentions.

A source conflict must remain visible and the affected role must not be accepted as settled. Empty candidate lists mean no_candidate, not proven absence. No whole-notice ready label is permitted unless every required in-scope value is covered and required absences are independently established by a supported runtime method. Post-run gold knowledge may assess completeness, but must not be fed back to promote the runtime decision.

The final governing runtime issue and merge policy is recorded in `RUNTIME-POLICY-OVERRIDE.json`; it overrides broader proposer diagnostic hints. Freeze the terminal-selection rule before running: how accepted candidates are deduplicated, how multiple valid destinations/deadlines are retained, how fallback replaces or conflicts with provisional native results, and whether native decisions remain provisional until document processing ends. Score the actual final terminal set; retain all intermediate states and separately report any earlier unsafe value exposed as accepted. Do not choose the best representation retrospectively. Duplicate mentions of the same role/value count once, while materially different values or conditions remain separate.

## Independent reference labels

For each full notice, annotate all relevant written-comment close dates and direct submission emails, including alternatives and their roles, with page/region and exact literal evidence. For dates record explicit components, source precision, applicable conditions and whether absolute/unconditional and unambiguous within supported scope. Record source states as present_in_scope, present_out_of_scope, not_stated, explicit_negative or unresolved_conflict/uncertainty. Preserve printed conflicting values rather than guessing legal precedence. Group repeated mentions of the same fact; do not inflate recall with duplicates.

The independent reviewer checks every retained page, boundaries, continuations, footers and neighboring material before labels are READY. Source-only labels must not know what the pipeline proposed or accepted. After runtime, compare original-source truth, parser fidelity, candidate availability, normalization and semantic decisions as separate stages. Re-review only as an explicit versioned correction when necessary, preserving the original reference.

## Metrics and progression gate

The denominator for field coverage is the complete set of source-resolvable, in-scope DATE and EMAIL field-value instances across all 12 notices. Count every distinct applicable value once; if the task permits multiple submission emails, all belong in recall. Missing candidates, provider failures and budget stops remain unrecovered instances. Report date and email denominators and coverage separately as well as pooled. A field type with no source-resolvable in-scope examples is insufficient evidence for that field, not 100 percent performance.

Report candidate recall against original source and, separately, conditional on faithful parser evidence; exact normalized-value accuracy among proposals; text-support decisions versus source fidelity; wrong-role/condition errors; unsafe accepted claims; accepted correct field coverage; no-candidate and unsupported abstention; native acceptance, OCR escalation and post-OCR acceptance; and source-conflict routing. Audit every shadow-accepted critical value against source images, and every source-labeled required value for completeness. Give complete notice counts and paired stage traces, not just successful cases.

Document completeness is separate from field coverage: a source-complete notice covers all required in-scope dates/emails, preserves conflicts and does not claim missing fields absent. Also report the pipeline's own readiness declarations and false readiness; gold-computed completeness is an evaluation result, not a runtime capability. Include all 12 scheduled notices with operational and unsupported cases shown. A correct abstention on a relative date does not become recovered absolute-date evidence.

Adopt the primary's small-sample progression rule: zero source-incorrect shadow-accepted critical values, zero accepted unresolved conflicts, and at least 25 percent of source-resolvable in-scope date/email field-value instances correctly shadow-accepted, with all 12 notices accounted for and quotas disclosed. A wrong role, lost condition, unsupported repaired string, out-of-scope claim or unauditable accepted value is not a correct acceptance; report it as unsafe and fail the zero-unsafe requirement. If the pooled denominator is zero, or a target field has no eligible examples, report insufficient evidence for that scope. Report the pooled threshold alongside both field-specific coverages; do not imply both independently reached 25 percent unless they did. This gate permits consideration of a larger shadow evaluation only. Twelve selected notices cannot certify production reliability or a low rare-error rate.

## Resource accounting

Hard caps are 12 scheduled notices, 120 Jev candidate cases total across native and fallback channels, 60 submitted OCR PDF pages and a nominal estimated combined budget of $1. Count each candidate-channel evaluation as a case, including repeat values evaluated after OCR. Four questions in one case are four judgments but one case; report both. Batch requests do not hide cases. Reserve estimated cost before each request and account for all scheduled/in-flight work. Stop before a call would exceed a case/page/estimate cap; retain remaining work as budget_stop rather than quietly excluding it.

No automatic POST retry. Durable request intent, input/code/prompt hashes, model identifiers, received responses, exact scored text, usage, timestamps and terminal state must be retained. An uncertain call or missing usage requiring reconciliation stops new calls; unknown cost is never zero. OCR page charges include the entire submitted PDF, not just pages containing candidates. Requested model/engine aliases do not prove an underlying release; report returned versions and unknowns honestly. Keep list-price estimates, provider-reported charge and invoice totals distinct. Record native, OCR, Jev, wrapper and review time separately. No new paid parsing or timing repetitions are authorized beyond the bounded pipeline path.
