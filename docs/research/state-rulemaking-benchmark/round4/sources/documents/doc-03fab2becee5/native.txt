            TOPICS OF RULES PROPOSED TO BE REPEALED AND ADOPTED

      The Chief Election Officer proposes to repeal Hawaii Administrative Rules, Title
3, Chapters 171 through 176, and adopt Hawaii Administrative Rules Title 3, Chapter
177.

       The repeal of Title 3, Chapters 171 through 176, and adoption of Title 3, Chapter
177 is necessary to address the migration to elections by mail, changes in the
administration of elections, and to make housekeeping changes.

       The subjects involved in the repeal of Title 3, Chapters 171 through 176 are as
follows:

       a.     The process for the adoption, amendment, or repeal of rules by the Chief
Election Officer (Chapter 171).

       b.      Voting procedures (Chapter 172). The chapter includes 12 subchapters of
rules covering the following areas: (1) general provisions; (2) voter education; (3) voter
registration; (4) voter challenges and appeals; (5) political parties; (6) precinct officials
and watchers; (7) ballot printing and delivery; (8) voting procedures at polling places; (9)
vote disposition; (10) presidential petitions; and (11) access to government records.

       c.      Nomination paper procedures (Chapter 173).

       d.      Absentee voting procedures (Chapter 174).

       e.      Elections by mail procedures (Chapter 175).

       f.      Voting systems (Chapter 176).

       The subjects involved in the adoption of Title 3, Chapters 177 are as follows:

       a.      Establish and clarify process for the adoption, amendment, or repeal of
rules by the Chief Election Officer (Subchapter 1). The proposed rules include the
following: (1) initiation of the adoption, amendment, or repeal of rules by the Chief
Election Officer; (2) petitions for the adoption, amendment, or repeal of rules; (3)
adoption, amendment, or repeal of rules; (4) conduct of hearing; (5) the decision and
taking effect of rules; and (6) emergency rule making.
        b.     Establish and clarify general provisions (Subchapter 2). The proposed
rules include the following: (1) purpose and general applicability of the rules; (2)
construction of laws; (3) election officials; (4) office of the clerk; (5) office of the chief
election officer; (6) format of election documents; (7) receipt, filing, or accessibility of
election documents; (8) districts, precincts, polling places, and ballot types; (9)
Saturday, deadlines; (10) postponement of elections; natural disaster; (11)
compensation for election day officials; and (12) security of ballots and election
supplies.

       c.     Establish and clarify procedures relating to voter education (Subchapter
3). The proposed rules include the following: (1) purpose; (2) voter pamphlet; and (3)
election equipment loans.

       d.      Establish and clarify procedures relating to voter registration (Subchapter
4). The proposed rules include the following: (1) voter registration and related forms;
(2) voter registration forms; distribution; (3) voter registration forms; collection; (4) voter
registration forms; electronic transmission by government agencies; (5) transfer or
change of registration initiated by the voter; (6) clerk's approval; when voter becomes
registered; (7) determination of residence; (8) National Voter Registration Act of 1993,
as amended; general program to remove ineligible voters, using change of address
information supplied by the postal service; (9) transfer or change of registration initiated
by the clerk; (10) confidential registration; and (11) voter registration information;
prohibited uses.

       e.      Establish and clarify procedures relating to voter challenges and appeals
(Subchapter 5). The proposed rules include the following: (1) prerequisites and
grounds for challenge; (2) challenges in writing not occurring in a voter service center;
procedure; (3) appeal to the board from a clerk’s ruling on a challenge in writing not
occurring in a voter service center; (4) challenge at the voter service center; procedure;
(5) appeal to the board from decision at the voter service center; (6) challenge to
confidential registration; special procedure; (7) correction of errors; appeal to the board
of registration from clerk's ruling; and (8) challenged ballot; electronic.

      f.    Establish and clarify procedures relating to access to government records
(Subchapter 6). The proposed rule addresses access to election documents; general
procedures.

        g.     Establish and clarify procedures relating to presidential petitions
(Subchapter 7). The proposed rules include the following: (1) presidential petitions;
issuing; (2) presidential petitions; withdrawal of signatures; and (3) presidential petitions;
verification of signatories.

        h.     Establish and clarify procedures relating to political parties (Subchapter 8).
The proposed rules include the following: (1) political parties; qualification; (2) party
petitions; issuing; (3) party petitions; withdrawal of signatures; and (4) party petitions;
verification of signatories.
         i.     Establish and clarify procedures relating to nomination papers
(Subchapter 9). The proposed rules include the following: (1) nomination papers; when
available, application, and format; (2) nomination papers; withdrawal of signature; (3)
nomination papers; verification of signatories; (4) nomination papers; candidate name
on ballot; (5) nomination papers; candidate’s name; office and district; political party
affiliation or nonpartisan designation; and (6) filing fee; payment of.

      j.      Establish and clarify procedures relating to ballots (Subchapter 10). The
proposed rules include the following: (1) ballot definition; (2) ballot design and
arrangement; candidate vacancies; (3) ballots for voter service centers; (4)
accountability and security of ballots; and (5) electronic voting systems; marking of
marksense ballot.

       k.      Establish and clarify procedures relating to voter service centers and
places of deposit, generally (Subchapter 11). The proposed rules include the following:
(1) voter service center; general operation and services; (2) voter service center;
security and regulation of activities; (3) voter service center; collection of voting
materials; (4) voter service center; return of materials; (5) voter service center; officials;
(6) voter service center; watchers; (7) places of deposit; generally; (8) voter service
center and place of deposit; campaign activities; and (9) voter service center and place
of deposit; non-campaign activities.

       l.      Establish and clarify procedures relating to voter service centers and
places of deposit, voting procedure (Subchapter 12). The proposed rules include the
following: (1) voter service center; verification of the identity of currently registered
voters; (2) voter service center; verification of the identity of applicants registering in
person to vote; (3) demonstration ballots; (4) voter service center; marksense ballots;
spoiled; and (5) provisional voting; general procedures.

       m.      Establish and clarify procedures relating to ballot package procedures
(Subchapter 13). The proposed rules include the following: (1) ballot packages;
contents; eligibility; (2) ballot packages; timing of transmittal, generally; (3) ballot
packages; timing of transmittal, military and overseas voter requests; (4) ballot
packages; timing of transmittal, absentee requests; (5) ballot packages; timing of
transmittal, replacement requests to be transmitted by mail; (6) ballot packages; timing
of transmittal, in-person pick up; (7) ballot packages; timing of transmittal, replacement
requests to be transmitted by electronic transmission; and (8) ballot packages;
replacement and absentee requests, form.

        n.     Establish and clarify procedures relating to return identification envelope
procedures (Subchapter 14). The proposed rules include the following: (1) return and
receipt of return identification envelopes; manner, location, and time of receipt; (2)
return identification envelopes; general preparing of ballots for counting; (3) return
identification envelopes; signature validation; (4) return identification envelopes;
signature device; and (5) return identification envelopes; notification and correction of
deficiencies.

       o.     Establish and clarify procedures relating to voting systems (Subchapter
15). The proposed rules include the following: (1) voting system requirements; (2)
approval of all voting equipment, materials, and procedures; (3) county elections;
assistance; (4) elections services; provision and charges; (5) testing the vote counting
system; electronic voting systems; (6) experimental voting systems; rules and
procedures; (7) electronic voting systems; generally; and (8) electronic voting systems;
marksense voting system.

        p.     Establish and clarify procedures relating to vote disposition (Subchapter
16). The proposed rules include the following: (1) electronic voting systems;
documentation of overages and underages; records; (2) electronic voting systems;
centralized and decentralized counting; (3) electronic voting systems; marksense voting
system; disposition of marks; (4) electronic voting systems, transmission, receipt, and
tabulation of votes; (5) electronic voting systems; marksense ballot voting system
results; (6) electronic voting systems; direct recording electronic voting system results;
(7) electronic voting systems; inspection, audit, and experimental testing; (8) electronic
voting system; counting center procedures; (9) electronic voting system centralized
counting; receipt at counting center; (10) electronic voting system counting; duplication
procedure; (11) electronic voting system decentralized counting; receipt at counting
center; (12) electronic voting system; handling of vote data storage media; (13)
electronic voting system; auditing; (14) electronic voting system; recount; (15)
challenged voter's ballot; disposition of at counting center; (16) election results;
certification of; (17) certificate of election and certificate of results, form; (18) processing
of voted ballots at the counting center; return identification envelopes; centralized
counting; (19) processing of uncounted voted ballots from voter service centers; and
(20) reconciliation of voted ballots.

       q.     Establish and clarify procedures relating to paper ballot voting system
(Subchapter 17). The proposed rules include the following: (1) paper ballot voting
system; voting procedure at the voter service center; and (2) paper ballot voting system;
counting ballots.

testimony at the hearing.
