## Compact source contract

- The compact form must retain all source functions: identity/context, current participation, future regulated/adjudicatory duties, background, endpoint purpose, modality, conditions, qualifications and complete relationships. Compactness cannot be achieved by silently excluding inconvenient claims or source regions.
- Evidence IDs are created deterministically by the host from the frozen source; the model selects IDs rather than inventing character offsets. Verify complete source preservation, ordering, repeated-text identity, empty/separator handling and Unicode fidelity in the host evidence table.
- Source reconstruction must be exact and hash-bound. Evidence IDs may locate support; they cannot by themselves prove that an assertion's full meaning follows from a span or that every source claim was discovered.
- Enumerate every default and omitted field. Defaults must be unambiguous, explicitly documented and unable to turn unknown/uncertain information into asserted certainty. Preserve uncertainty and distinguish absent, empty and defaulted fields where their meanings differ.
