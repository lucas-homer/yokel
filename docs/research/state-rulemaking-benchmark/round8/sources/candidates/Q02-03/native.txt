                                         Notice of Hearing

The Department of Natural Resources announces that it will hold a public hearing on a
permanent rule to revise chs. NR 12, 16, 19, 21, 22, 27, 40 and 45 relating to amphibian and
reptile regulations, at the time and place shown below.

Hearing Information

Date:   January 12, 2026

Time: 5:00 p.m.

Virtual Location Link: Join the meeting now

Rule Information: Current rules establish provisions for a variety of activities involving
Wisconsin’s native amphibians and reptiles, including harvest, live take, possession,
propagation, purchase, sale, trade, exhibition, rehabilitation, stocking and release. However, the
majority of these rules have not been updated since the 1990s. Additionally, the department has
heard feedback that these rules are difficult to interpret and enforce. These rules will be updated
to provide better clarity to the public while better protecting the amphibians and reptiles.
Poaching, harassment, increased collection pressure and wildlife trafficking are negatively
impacting amphibian and reptile populations throughout North America and around the world. In
addition, many emerging diseases are also causing increased pressure on amphibian and reptile
populations

Appearances at the Hearing and Submittal of Written Comments

The public has the opportunity to testify at the hearing. Registration will take place at the hearing
by completing a Hearing Appearance form, which is available here Hearing Appearance form.
Pre-registration is strongly encouraged if you plan to provide spoken comments during the
hearing. To pre-register, please download and complete the fillable Hearing Appearance form
and send it to scottr.karel@wisconsin.gov.

Comments on the proposed rule must be received on or before January 13, 2026. Written
comments may be submitted by U.S. mail, E-mail, or through the internet and will have the same
weight and effect as oral statements presented at the public hearing.
