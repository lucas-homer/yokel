The hearing will be held for the sole purpose of gathering public comments on the Proposed Rules (47 Ill. Reg.
15559; November 3, 2023) implementing the Paid Leave for All Workers Act (56 Ill. Adm. Code 200). Persons
interested in presenting testimony at this hearing are advised that the Department of Labor will adhere to the
following procedures in the conduct of the hearing:
  a) Participants wishing to testify should sign up to testify at https://forms.office.com/g/59x8r6rMqv to sign
     up prior to the hearing, whether attending virtually or in-person. This is distinct from WebEx registration.
  b) No oral testimony shall exceed an aggregate of 5 minutes for the presentation.
  c) No person will be recognized to speak for a second time until all persons wishing to testify have done so.
  d) Witnesses are encouraged to submit their remarks in writing to DOLPaidLeave@illinois.gov before or
     after the hearing.
  e) Those individuals who are unable to attend the public hearing but wish to comment on the Proposed Rule
     should submit written comments on or before December 18, 2023.
     The Department of Labor is committed to ensuring that this public hearing meets the disability access
     needs of all participants. Please contact DOL.PaidLeave@illinois.gov or call 217-558-1270 to make any
     disability access requests as early as possible.
