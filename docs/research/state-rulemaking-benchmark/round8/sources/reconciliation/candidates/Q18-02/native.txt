PROPOSED RULES:
     (1) LSA Document #26-21 – Insurance Holding Company Systems

     Subject Matter of the Rule
     The proposed rule amends 760 IAC 1-15.1 concerning the following updates: (1) Clarifying that
     the ultimate controlling person of an insurer shall furnish the required information on Form F. (2)
     Clarifying the requirements for cost sharing and management services agreements between
     affiliates regarding access to records and data, and adding language related to the group capital
     calculation requirement. (3) The criteria for the lead state commissioner to exempt an insurance
     holding company system from the group capital calculation filing requirement. (4) The criteria a
     non-U.S. jurisdiction uses to consider the group capital calculation recognized and accepted. IDOI
     seeks comment on the affected citations listed and any other provisions of Title 760 that may be
     affected by this rulemaking.
