# R8 preregistration — proposed for primary freeze

This fresh, bounded research experiment uses the reviewed R7 representation unchanged. It measures extraction omissions, claim correctness and paired complete-record verification; it does not establish national accuracy, human-gold accuracy or production eligibility. R6/R7 and all earlier sources, labels, code and results are immutable. This document/code do not release collection or paid execution; primary freezes the reviewed implementation and collection plan first.

## Cohort and freeze order

Target **8 complete fresh individual notices, at least 6 states, 6 general plus 2 natural conditional participation-timing/hearing-availability controls, at most 2 notices/state, at most 40 original PDF pages total and 30,000 native codepoints/notice**. Controls are chosen by source properties before outputs, never manufactured by editing notices. A control must contain actual conditional participation timing or hearing availability; format-practicability wording alone does not qualify. Negative lifecycle notices are excluded by the collection plan. Collection is official-source-only and deduplicates underlying notices against every earlier round; retain all pages/native bytes and provenance. No OCR, clipping, repair, whole-register substitutes, cherry-picking easy fields, replacement after labeling or replacement after failed admission. Collection shortfalls are retained and reported, never padded with invented observations.

Freeze sequence: (1) primary-reviewed pipeline/runtime/protocol/model/cost settings and collection plan; (2) collected source bytes, native text, page images and actual schedule; (3) independent source-first AI image-reference labels for every scheduled notice, all pages, plus labeler provenance/receipt and hashes; (4) only then source-only inventory and paired model calls. Labeler sees images and minimal document/hash/page identifiers only, not substantive collector conclusions, native extraction, proposed records or model outputs. No fresh-source retuning, trial calls, best-of-N, paid repair or verifier-assisted edits. Any protocol change requires a new preregistration before reading fresh outputs; do not quietly change the frozen version.

## Fixed semantic universe and independent audit

Reuse the R6/R7 image-reference and audit field shapes with R8 evaluator namespace. Seven categories: fact, relationship, instruction, disclosure, ancillary, identity, uncertainty. Include participation phases/timing, submission and hearing-registration/access routes, alternatives and qualifications; instruction modality and applicability; separately typed consideration/publication/public-posting/confidentiality disclosures and exceptions; ancillary offices, addresses, helper links, phone/endpoint relationships and actual functions; notice identity and uncertainty. Preserve component and group claims, exact evidence, date-only precision and explicit conditions. No inferred year/timezone/deadline arithmetic. Exclude substantive program obligations, ordinary citations and routine publication metadata unless they identify or qualify participation; record exclusions before outputs.

Source semantic IDs are independent of extractor IDs. Each image unit has page/region/quote, value if explicit, role/modality/targets and uncertainty. Repeated identical meaning may be one unit with multiple citations; different roles/phases/conditions remain separate. Full-notice domain absence requires every page reviewed. Unresolved notice identity/controlling scope or missing continuation are acceptance blockers; a faithfully represented conditional event is not automatically a blocker. These are **independent AI image references, not human-adjudicated gold**. Freeze a page-view ledger and source-scope decisions before extraction. A JSON assertion alone does not prove blinding; primary retains the temporal and access receipts.

After execution, independently enumerate **every claim** in each available raw inventory and assembled record, including extra claims, coverage assertions, components, group relations, disclosure typing and applicability. Map **every frozen source unit** to matched/omitted/mismatched/unknown, with claim references and rationale; exact quote matching alone is insufficient. Evidence-only prose receives no structured recovery credit. A relationship cannot match while a required endpoint is omitted. Null artifacts get null assessments and remain unserved; an audited empty object is not a substitute. Verifier self-scores are never audit truth. All unsupported/incorrect/unknown additions prevent complete support.

## Frozen paired decision

R7 pipeline/schema/prompt semantics and model request builders remain unchanged and hash-pinned. A source-only inventory sees full native source and no labels, candidates or prior outputs. Deterministic assembly retains every fact or returns null; no partial-record acceptance. Both verifier bodies are admitted before either call, with identical canonical full-source+record payload, hashes and five common dimensions: support, association, requirements, source_identity, completeness. Jev receives one five-Choice batch; the stronger verifier assesses exactly every item including components/groups. Stronger explanations have no Jev equivalent; report this asymmetry and shared model-family limitations.

Shadow acceptance requires a valid completed response covering the entire frozen assessment schedule, every common dimension favorable, and no deterministic uncertainty/review reason. `source_coverage_complete` means the complete response schedule was assessed, including when judgments are negative; it is not source truth or favorable completeness. Confidence is diagnostic only; no new threshold. Independent source correctness/completeness determines useful acceptance, without promoting model verdicts. All artifacts remain action_ready=false and production_eligible=false.

## Requests, budget and durable stop rules

**Nominal/conservative budget guard <=$2 and <=24 attempted calls**: <=8 inventory, <=8 Jev and <=8 stronger, no retries, fallbacks or extra paid probes. Models/settings/caps remain R6/R7: OpenRouter `openai/gpt-6.1-sol` (allowed canonical response ID `openai/gpt-6.1-sol-20260929`), low reasoning, regular OpenAI only; 6,144 inventory and 4,096 stronger completion tokens; conservative serialized-byte estimate +1,024 <=35,000 for each OpenRouter request. Jev `jev-1.13.0`: total estimate <=64,000 and state+longest question+1,024 <=32,000. Pricing/reservation is pinned, not a claim about current market prices or guaranteed model alias weights. Worst-case nominal reservation is $1.960704. No cap increase, clipping, chunking, question/fact dropping or unreviewed schema compression; honest size rejection remains in the denominator.

Reuse R6 durable ledger mechanics under R8-owned paths: persist request/identity and fsynced intent before POST; retain raw response, HTTP status/hash, latency, parsed result and usage; bind resume to exact pins, request and identity. Reparse completed raw responses without a POST. Intent without a confirmed response is an uncertain attempted call, charged conservatively; never automatically retry. A latched failure/uncertain intent stops further calls until primary reconciliation; do not skip to another notice or relabel the attempt uncalled. Before each POST reserve and account sum(max(reservation, known observed cost)); retain unknown costs as null and stop before additional spend if budget/caps/reconciliation block. The $2 reservation/stop guard is not an invoice guarantee: a response-reported charge can exceed its reservation after the call. Preserve that actual charge, latch the overrun and stop before any further call. A completed earlier arm stays completed if the next arm fails or is never dispatched. Credential handling is runtime/primary-owned and never enters sources, prompts, retained artifacts or protocol tooling.

## Observations and evaluation

`CONTRACT.json` and `evaluate.py` define R8 input versions and strict states. Reuse hash-pinned R7 scoring without changing metrics; a local isolated import replaces only its observation validator with R8's live-state validator and adapts top-level version tags. R7 record/inventory/payload schema IDs and hashes remain intact. This explicit adapter does not alter judgments, source labels, artifact hashes, denominators or costs.

A document has runtime gate and two independent arm states. Local source/admission/assembly gates never imply provider failures. `not_started` means no attempted call for that arm. A durable intent represents an attempted/possibly sent POST for conservative called accounting; unknown network outcome is recorded separately as transport_uncertain, not confirmed HTTP failure. Confirmed non-200 provider HTTP status is counted as provider_service_error under this protocol's operational definition; it is **not necessarily an outage** (auth/rate-limit errors remain distinguishable by status). Transport uncertainty, invalid structured response/usage and local stops are separate classes. Failed/uncalled arms have no invented judgment. A local pause can preserve a completed Jev judgment while stronger remains uncalled. A successfully parsed response that trips the after-call budget guard uses postresponse_budget_stop: retain its inventory/judgment, bind the receipt to the completed result and budget latch, and stop before any later call. Every failure/pause needs a hashed operational receipt; live runtime review must verify its binding to actual ledger artifacts, not trust a self-attested hash.

All collected/scheduled notices remain in denominators, including no extraction, null records, uncertain scope and missing observations. Missing observation rows are explicitly counted as unserved, never proof of a particular reached gate. Report gate/failure-class and called/uncalled counts separately from judgments. Descriptive rates use actual cohort size; the planned eight-notice gate remains false on shortfall. Whole-cohort completeness rates are unavailable when reference scope or available-artifact audits are incomplete. Claim/unit counts are clustered within notices, not independent statistical samples. Zero accepted implies null unsafe fraction, not demonstrated safety.

Per-arm success gate: adequate 8-notice/6-state/6-general/2-control/40-page cohort, every scheduled source independently complete, **zero unsafe accepted and >=2 correctly complete accepted records**. Report paired per-notice outcomes and all-scheduled useful coverage; never best-of-two union. Costs separate provider-reported, usage-estimated and conservative charges. Experiment total counts extraction once; each per-arm deployment scenario includes full extraction plus that arm. Cost per correct complete accepted record is null when its denominator is zero. Invoice verification, human review time and population accuracy remain unmeasured.
