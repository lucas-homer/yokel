## Questions the frozen design and fixtures must answer

- **Can exclusions conceal participation information?** Require a prospective, source-grounded inclusion/exclusion/uncertain-scope rule and auditable reasons/evidence. Reject exclusion merely because material is background-labeled, optional/advisory, absent from the record, difficult to represent, duplicated elsewhere, or inconvenient for size. Necessary proceeding identity/purpose, deadlines, access, conditions, ancillary roles and participation policies cannot vanish under a blanket “substantive context” exclusion. A declared complete span or populated exclusion list does not prove exhaustive discovery.
- **Is discovery independent of the candidate record?** Freeze source scope before inspecting the record or generated outputs. Same source plus different records must produce the same reference universe and scope decisions; adding a source-only obligation must change that universe despite unchanged candidate IDs. Audit the whole source independently for units never generated, including rejected/excluded material. Neither a model's exclusions nor its completeness claim can be its own oracle.
- **Are mixed passages split without losing qualifications?** Test a paragraph containing genuine background plus an instruction, and an instruction whose condition is supplied by adjacent background. Exclude only the irrelevant assertion, retaining the instruction and its necessary context. Atomic units must preserve links among separately lossy facts; merging them cannot hide partial loss or inflate sample counts.
- **Do endpoints retain their exact roles?** Separately represent actual destinations and their components, role, channel, applicability and association. Generic “mail/email/web” labels do not cover named destinations. Query-only telephone contact is not a submission route; a shared contact may support several distinct roles only with evidence. Test role swaps, omitted destinations, multiple addresses/phones and compound contact text while preserving correctly typed positives.
- **Do modality and qualifications survive?** Preserve mandatory, advisory, optional and conditional force, actor, purpose, exceptions and timing. Conditions must retain the force within them. An unclear record modality cannot recover a definite source instruction; an unquantified deadline or retained lack of timezone must not become an invented date or a new omission. Evidence/coverage text still cannot substitute for an operative assertion.
- **Are alternatives distinguished from separate permissions?** Require source support for interchangeability within the same purpose, scope and qualifications. Co-listing alone proves neither an alternative relationship nor its absence. Test full alternatives versus a valid subgroup, separately supported view/access permissions that need no additional grouping assertion, and explicitly stated choices whose relationship is actually lost. Do not make one representation mandatory when equivalent operative meaning survives.
- **Do unresolved states block acceptance?** Uncertain scope cannot default to excluded, and source/reference/role ambiguity cannot become matched through repetition. Preserve localized unknowns and dependency closure; unknowns block completeness while known defects remain visible. Missing or incomplete independent review must stay unverified. A valid schema or all included units matched cannot certify the unreviewed remainder.
- **Are unsupported record additions checked separately?** Source-to-record coverage alone cannot establish whole-record fidelity. Require an independently reviewed reverse check of every operative field, including compound text, unsupported endpoints/mandates and wrong roles, before any broader acceptance claim. If outside this round's scope, that limitation must explicitly block whole-record acceptance.
