# Participation scope and contact role refinement

This offline refinement specifies how to distinguish participation losses from background omissions without throwing away source evidence. It includes a source-first annotation contract, executable synthetic checks, and a prospective comparison plan. It does not change the live runner or production schema, and makes no new model-performance claim.

The proposed processing sequence is complete source inventory, source-only scope annotation, then matching to operative record fields. A separate reverse audit checks unsupported record additions. Scope annotation cannot delete inventory items or depend on what the candidate record happens to contain.

| Decision | Refined rule |
|---|---|
| Participation information | Capture opportunities, timing, actual destinations, contact purposes, procedures, conditions, modality and participation policies, including optional or advisory information. |
| Identifying context | Preserve the proceeding identity and rule transitions; listing chapter numbers cannot substitute for repeal or recreation assertions. |
| Background | Retain source-supported substantive effects and rationale separately. Their absence is not automatically a participation defect. |
| Unresolved scope | Preserve uncertainty and block a completeness result. A heading or difficulty of representation cannot justify exclusion. |
| Contact roles | Separate the actual endpoint from each purpose. A query contact does not automatically accept comments; ambiguous shared blocks stay unresolved. |
| Relationships | Preserve complete source-supported choices and their qualifications. Two independent viewing permissions need no invented alternatives group. Equivalent operative representations can pass. |
| Known losses plus uncertainty | Report both. Unknown interpretation blocks acceptance without erasing a separately established loss. |
| Reverse audit | Bind review to the exact source and operative values. A review of an older version cannot certify a changed record. |

These rules address the previous Wisconsin diagnostic without changing its original labels or denominators. They are prospective research rules, not a new legal interpretation of ambiguous source text.

## Implementation and review

The child task produced [the specification](protocol/SPEC.md), [machine-readable policy](protocol/policy.json), [offline validator](protocol/validator.py), and synthetic fixtures. Primary verification reproduced all 35 fixture expectations and 21 unittest methods on the revised candidate.

Independent initial review found four executable gaps: lost known-defect diagnostics under uncertainty, stale reverse-audit acceptance, contradictory scope annotations passing, and malformed operative assertions being accepted. Targeted regressions reproduced each failure before fixes. The [resolution](protocol/RESOLUTION.md) records the changes; the original candidate and initial review remain preserved for comparison.

The [final independent review](review/REVIEW.md) approved the offline annotation contract after 54 targeted checks passed and all five findings were resolved. Primary verification checked the final artifact hashes and all 6,771 historical files remain unchanged.

These tests check supplied annotations, not semantic discovery. Humans or models can still misclassify natural language or omit facts. Exact evidence and valid structure do not establish exhaustive source coverage or whole-record fidelity. Production readiness remains false.

## Next evaluation and unattended operation

The [comparison plan](protocol/comparison-plan.json) separates sealed reference answers from operational annotations and records exposure and human effort. Its proposed pilot compares a human-assisted source-first system with an unassisted direct verifier. That is a calibration design, not evidence of an automated ordering advantage or an unattended solution. An automated scope classifier remains unimplemented and unevaluated; replacing the annotator will need a separately frozen and reviewed model protocol against independent source references.

For the eventual unattended path, the target remains source discovery, source-only scope judgment, field matching, and independent reverse checks, with escalation for unresolved cases. This refinement settles the contract those components must meet; it does not select a classifier vendor or claim deterministic code can interpret source meaning.
