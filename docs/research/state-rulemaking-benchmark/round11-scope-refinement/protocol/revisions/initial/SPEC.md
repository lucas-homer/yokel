# Source discovery, scope and semantic contracts — offline review candidate

This research specification refines round 10; it authorizes no execution. No live compatibility claim, provider runtime, production readiness, action readiness, budget spend or state-jurisdiction production expansion is implied. All earlier artifacts, Wisconsin audit labels, original denominators and unknowns remain unchanged. The validator checks supplied annotations; it does not discover facts, interpret prose, adjudicate equivalence or measure a model.

## Three separate responsibilities

1. **Discovery A:** read the complete immutable source and available reference material, without any candidate record, candidate IDs, prior outputs, labels or defects. Preserve all source assertions potentially relevant to participation, identifying context, substantive background or unresolved scope. Split independently lossy assertions, not just paragraphs. Retain exact quotes and occurrence selectors, source/ref hashes and uncertainty. Missing/disputed reference material is an explicit limitation. Freeze the inventory before any scope or record matching. Do not shrink it to fit a request. Overflow or discovery omissions leave source completeness unverified.
2. **Source-only scope annotation:** annotate every frozen unit with category, source-relative basis, rationale and exact evidence. This layer cannot mutate, add, delete, merge or renumber inventory units, or inspect the candidate. Discovery corrections require a new version and retained diff before matching, never silent repair. Categories are `required_participation`, `identifying_context`, `retained_background`, `unresolved_scope`. Preserve background in the inventory and report it separately; “not required for participation” means a different reporting obligation, not erased evidence. Record presence, heading, convenience, representability, optional force, fixture expectations and match outcomes are forbidden exclusion rationales.
3. **Matching B:** use identical full source, frozen A, frozen scope and the complete operative candidate. Match all units, including background, with original judgments retained. Required-participation and identifying-context units govern scoped coverage. B cannot modify either source universe or scope. A separate reverse audit examines unsupported candidate additions; source-to-record matching cannot replace it.

Scope classification requires semantic judgment. This candidate uses a source-only independent human adjudicator plus deterministic policy checks and therefore adds no automatic paid model stage. A future model classifier would require separate authorization, cost accounting and source-only isolation review. The Python code has no keyword classifier or fixture-ID lookup pretending to supply those judgments. An independently reviewed reference universe must be discovered from the whole source, before seeing A/B or record outputs; crosswalk it against A afterward to detect never-generated units. Hash attestations only bind supplied annotations; they cannot prove who reviewed them, blindness, truth or exhaustive discovery.

## Required source-relative facts

Preserve the rulemaking agency, proceeding/docket/order identifiers, rule subject sufficient to distinguish the proceeding, and each identifying rule transition with its affected object (for example repeal NR 107, repeal NR 109, recreate NR 107 are three assertions). An identifier alone cannot recover its transition. Do not expand every substantive policy detail into a required identity fact.

Required participation includes every source-supported hearing/testimony/comment opportunity; dates, clocks, timezone stated or missing, cutoffs, receipt/postmark semantics and time changes; named destinations and access components; submission, attendance, oral testimony, questions, accommodation and ancillary contacts; eligibility, registration, forms, procedure, participation policies, required components, actor, conditions, exceptions and modality; access/viewing permissions and supported relationships. Keep the small-business coordinator when it serves an ancillary contact function even under regulatory-analysis text. Contacts are in scope by function, not position or heading. Background that supplies an instruction's condition, actor or applicability is retained as required context, not discarded as rationale.

Pure substantive effects, economic/regulatory analysis and policy rationale are `retained_background` when their loss changes no identifying or participation function. Exact evidence and rationale accompany that judgment. A mixed paragraph must split into background and participation/context assertions with necessary links. A deadline embedded in background prose remains required. Neither absence from the candidate nor already preserved generic topic text changes scope. The mechanical policy disallows known timing, instruction, role and relationship units as background; mislabeling prose as a background kind still requires independent review and is not mechanically detectable.

If source-relative function, shared-contact role, referenced content or relevant assertion boundary cannot be resolved, choose `unresolved_scope`, retain alternatives/reason and block completeness. Uncertainty is not permission to exclude. Unknowns do not erase separately known defects. Do not invent a quantitative deadline from “in advance,” a timezone from agency location, or a hidden form URL from its label.

## Atomic endpoint and role contract

An `endpoint` preserves one actual destination or access literal; split independently lossy address lines, telephone number, code, URL, recipient and other components into separate units when needed. A separate `endpoint_role` has exactly one endpoint and one purpose, references its endpoint as a dependency, and preserves modality, condition and qualifications. Purpose choices are in policy.json; the same address supporting submissions and questions requires two role units. Generic “mail/email/web” cannot recover actual destinations. Scope/function ambiguities remain unresolved rather than inferred from a medium.

An expressly query-only synthetic phone is not a comment-submission route. Wisconsin's shared “written comments and questions should be submitted to” contact block contains phone/address/email; it is not fresh universal proof of a query-only phone or permission to submit comments by telephone. Preserve prior audit labels unchanged; independently annotate new shared-block ambiguities as unknown when the source cannot establish purpose. Accommodation request contacts are not interchangeable with question contacts.

Conditions retain force **within** the condition: “if speaking, preregistration strongly encouraged” is advisory for intended speakers, not mandatory or unconditional. Preserve `mandatory`, `advisory`, `optional`, `prohibited`, `descriptive`, `unknown`; keep qualifications such as registration, geography, actor, channel restrictions and material version. “Should” cannot silently become “must.” Atomicity means one independently lossy assertion, not a guarantee that a host can recognize bundled natural language. An `instruction` string can still be compound; independent review must inspect it.

## Relationships and alternatives

Relationship members refer to role units, not naked phone literals. Preserve the full member set, same purpose, applicability, modality and qualifications. Use separate relation types: `substitution`, `independent_permissions`, `joint_requirement`, `sequence`, `applies_to`. A substitution claim requires exact source evidence supporting functional equivalence for that purpose and independent semantic adjudication. An evidence quote's presence does not prove it supports equivalence. Equivalence may be purpose-limited: Zoom and telephone may each permit attendance, while only one channel offers a particular testimony function. Do not imply stronger functions. Different registration/code conditions remain explicit rather than erased to make alternatives uniform.

Two independently stated permissions to view the same rule at site A and office B are recovered when both roles/permissions and applicable material identity/conditions survive. Mere “also,” co-listing or similarity does not require a new alternatives group, nor establish actual substitution. Positive fixtures pass with two independent role claims and no grouping. If source actually asserts substitution, a phone-only subgroup does not recover a Zoom-plus-two-phone relationship; co-preserved endpoints alone do not recover the assertion.

Matching concerns operative meaning rather than native layout. A source-supported relationship may survive in an explicit group **or** distributed operative instructions that explicitly preserve the same choice; a reviewer produces the same canonical semantic facet for either. This validator accepts a normalized annotated operative projection, not raw production records, and does not require a native group object. Member references use the frozen inventory namespace through a reviewer-established binding, never inference from readable candidate IDs. Canonical projection of relationship facets drops discovery `equivalence_evidence`; evidence is source support, not a required operative quotation. Qualifications, purpose, modality, conditions and full members remain operative facets. Reordering or equivalent wording requires prospective reviewer normalization before this exact-equality check; the code does not adjudicate language equivalence.

## Offline annotation envelope and gates

`policy.json` declares closed keys, enums and permitted scope categories by kind. `fixtures.json` contains complete private synthetic sources, frozen inventory, immutable classified-inventory witness, total scope and mapping annotations, normalized operative assertions, source-review attestation and separate record audit. Each evidence selector uses exact Unicode text and a positive 1-based occurrence among overlapping matches; no whitespace, OCR/glyph or Unicode repair. IDs and dependencies are unique, total and acyclic. References must resolve in the supplied operative projection. No evidence-only or coverage-only pointer can recover an operative assertion.

Each mapping is `matched`, `missing`, `mismatched` or `unknown`, with operative refs and a reason. Matched requires exact preservation of the normalized semantic facets, no own source/modality uncertainty and all dependencies matched. Missing has no recovery refs; mismatched has changed/partial operative recovery. Preserve raw mappings and separately report effective unknown propagation. If a relation is known incomplete and one member is ambiguous, retain that known defect in diagnostics while blocking overall completion for the unknown.

The scope gate is `unverified` if independent source review is absent, any unresolved-scope unit remains, or any required/context unit is effectively unknown; otherwise `not_complete` for known required losses; otherwise `all_required_inventoried_units_matched`. That last label is deliberately bounded to the reviewed inventory, not a mechanically certified whole-source result. Background absences remain visible but are not participation defects. A frozen source-review attestation binds exact UTF-8 source SHA256, canonical inventory and canonical scope hashes. “Reviewer attestation only” never means this code independently established completeness.

The reverse audit must enumerate **every** operative claim from the original candidate, including compound fields, and adjudicate supported, unsupported and unknown assertions with source evidence. It must preserve original/projection hashes and all identity bindings. Offline code verifies exhaustive review-ref enumeration against its supplied projection only; omission from that projection still needs independent review. `record_fidelity=unverified` when audit is not run or any reverse judgment is unknown, `not_faithful` for unsupported additions, otherwise only reviewer attestation. Required coverage can pass while fidelity fails. Never combine the two into an acceptance shortcut. Production readiness stays false and live compatibility unverified in every result.

## Reproduce and review

Run from this directory with existing Python 3:

```sh
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 validator.py
PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1 python3 test_validator.py
```

Optionally pass one annotated JSON case to validator.py. make_fixtures.py reproduces policy/fixture annotations for authoring; it is not a semantic engine and should not be rerun after freeze without a version/diff. fixture-results.json and test-output.txt retain execution evidence. Controls cover source-only additions with unchanged candidate, candidate-only mutations with unchanged source scope, exclusions hiding deadlines, mixed paragraphs and background-supplied conditions, multiple roles, query-only phone, shared-role uncertainty, accommodations, ancillary coordinator, advisory/conditional force, transitions, full versus subset alternatives, qualification losses, independent view permissions, glyph/reference closure and separate unsupported-addition audit.

READY.json freezes this review candidate and input hashes. It is not independent protocol approval, runtime release, semantic completeness, model evaluation, provider compatibility or production readiness. The primary must read and integrate it; this child does not message other chats.
